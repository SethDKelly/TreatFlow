# TreatFlow — Adopted Concept Family and Intentional Scope

**Authority:** [006-D decision evidence](../../phases/006/006-d-treatflow-execution.md). **The Phase 006-F review has exited with carry-forward.** This document owns deliberately adopted *conceptual design scope*, not software packages, launch commitments, released products, or approval to collect or disclose information. The [dependency owner](../dependencies/concept-dependence.md) owns contextual edge semantics; the [Phase 005 action surface](../composition/application-action-surface.md) and its C/D composition specifications own action behavior.

## Family anchor and chosen design scope

**Adopt one integrated TreatFlow-native observational/research concept family** with all five independent concepts in its design vocabulary: **Observation (O), Contribution Stewardship (S), Revision (V), Research Provision (R), Controlled Disclosure (D)**.

The adopted *design intent* is voluntary qualified Halloween observations, legitimate private contribution standing and correction, scientifically honest inquiry suitability, and an independently protected **possible** evidence-availability path. Including D does not mean releases must occur or that protective permission exists; denial/nonavailability is a meaningful outcome. Including R does not entail providing raw observations or performing Poisson inference. S and V remain optional for each eligible report, even though the full conceptual design includes them.

**Adopt as explicitly permitted restricted experience states within that family**, not separate supported products: reporting before any S/V association; no-delivery/insufficient-evidence research assessment; protected withholding/denial; protected historical correction with no past-copy repair. The presence of all five concepts at product-family level does not require all five to act on each occurrence.

**Scope exclusions:** separate product tiers, editions, package variants, public dashboards, unrestricted data dumps, mandatory identity registration, automatic recipient notification after correction, automated data retraction, visitor/person tracking, model confirmation as a product promise, commercial data brokerage and any real deployment plan.

## Native-family roles and dependency context

For the **adopted integrated native-evidence family**, S and V retain their [qualified dependency on O](../dependencies/concept-dependence.md). **R → O is now supported in the chosen native-evidence role**: the intended TreatFlow inquiry assesses evidence originating from its own Observation concept, *not* an independently imported evidence source. **D → O is supported for the chosen native-observation material availability role**: protective decisions under that role concern native observation material. These edges are contextual only; generic R can assess other evidence and generic D can protect unrelated material elsewhere. Neither R nor D implies S/V participation. D-02's joint release action has R and D as **guarded participants**, but neither concept universally depends on the other for inclusion; no R ↔ D edge is inferred.

The chosen **concept-family inclusion** of all five is an adopted purpose scope, not proof that each is a graph-theoretic prerequisite of O or that a smaller coherent subset is invalid. Phase 006-E will check whether each included concept's Phase 005 application actions remain coherent in no-delivery and withheld states.

## Protected guardrails and re-entry

No approval exists for collection, consent, guardian/minor participation, real-world correction/adjudication authority, identification of nonparticipants, safe geographic/timing disclosure, privacy risk thresholds, retention/withdrawal, model assumptions or feasible operator load. **Any real-world collection, processing or release is blocked pending separately established safeguards.**

If 006-E finds that selected roles imply an action absent from Phase 005, reopen 005. If an edge implies another concept's intrinsic private state, reopen 003/004. Phase 006-F independently challenges scope coherence. No UI, API, persistence, service graph or implementation authorization follows.

## 006-E variant-action coherence qualification

[006-E audit](../../phases/006/006-e-treatflow-execution.md) verifies the adopted full five-concept *design* with independent qualified observation reporting, optional protected standing/correction, research suitability without delivery, protected denial, and only conditionally coordinated D-02 release. These are **experience states inside one integrated family**, not separately adopted products or permission to collect/release information. Four contextual native inclusion edges (S/V/R/D → O) do not cause runtime actions or bypass concept-intrinsic guards. No blocking intrinsic defect or missing synchronization has been demonstrated under currently bounded promises; if a later mapped experience requires automatic notification, new public source access, or unmodeled protected action, reopen Phase 005 rather than inferring it from concept inclusion. Phase 006-F retains independent exit authority.
