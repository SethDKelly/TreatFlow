# TreatFlow — Contextual Concept Inclusion Dependencies

**Current decision owner:** [006-B evidence](../../phases/006/006-b-treatflow-execution.md). **Phase 006-F exited with carry-forward; the adopted TreatFlow-native contextual edges are S/V/R/D → O as qualified by the [current family owner](../project/concept-family-scope.md).** A directed `A → B` means that *in a specified application-family role*, A's inclusion makes sense only when B also participates. This is **not** an intrinsic reference, synchronization, data relationship, API, implementation dependency, scope commitment or assertion that B must occur before A.

## Application context

The currently analyzed family concerns voluntary Halloween **TreatFlow-native observation evidence**, contributor recognition/correction of that evidence, potential bounded inquiry qualification and controlled prospective information availability. Other applications of the same generic concepts may have different edges. The [five independent concept specifications](../concepts/candidates.md) and [Phase 005 application behavior](../composition/application-action-surface.md) remain the behavioral authorities.

## Currently supported *context-qualified* edges

| Extrinsic inclusion edge | Scope and application role rationale | Why this is not intrinsic |
|---|---|---|
| **Contribution Stewardship → Observation** | **Only for the TreatFlow-native contribution-standing experience:** Stewardship is being included to let participants recognize/contest responsibility for *observations contributed to TreatFlow*. Without Observation, that **particular application role** has no contributed evidence objects to stand for. | Generic Stewardship.Item can independently represent an item in other applications. No `ObservationId` is embedded in Stewardship, and neither its actions nor reporting require a Stewardship action. |
| **Revision → Observation** | **Only for the TreatFlow-native observational-correction experience:** Revision is being included to preserve historical factual standing of **TreatFlow-reported observations**. With no Observation, this specific project role has no observation assertion to correct. | Revision.Subject/Assertion remain abstract and Revision could independently manage other claims in another product family. No compulsory subject creation follows from including Observation. |

These accepted context-qualified edges hold within the **named native-evidence family**. They do **not** establish a universal relation over every coherent family using the five concepts, nor force any current product variant to include Stewardship or Revision. Phase 006-C tested representative subsets and alternate roles; its historical test remains linked below.

## Rejected candidate edges

| Candidate | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| **Observation → Contribution Stewardship** | Reporting remains meaningful without private responsibility association; requiring it violates low-burden participation (P-01). |
| **Observation → Revision** | A bounded qualified observation still has intelligible first-assertion meaning without an enabled correction-history capability. Choosing full correction support for an adopted scope is a separate question. |
| **Revision → Contribution Stewardship** | A factual amendment needs legitimately established authority but not necessarily contributor-standing state; authority and responsibility are distinct. |
| **Contribution Stewardship → Revision** | Recognizing/contesting contribution responsibility remains valuable without authorizing or making factual amendments. |
| **Research Provision → Controlled Disclosure** *(unqualified)* | A no-delivery research-fitness assessment can legitimately be inconclusive/unsuitable without disclosure entitlement; D-02 binds only **protected delivery**, not research assessment's whole existence. |
| **Controlled Disclosure → Research Provision** *(unqualified)* | Protected nonresearch prospective information availability can be meaningful without scientific inquiry qualification. |
| **Any edge solely because C-01–C-03 or D-01–D-03 synchronizes two concepts** | Composition of included actions is not a proof that one must always be included with the other. |

## Historical contextual-edge questions (resolved for adopted native role)

1. **Research Provision → Observation** in the native Halloween-evidence family may be justified when the intended role is evaluating TreatFlow-collected evidence. But generic Research Provision can assess externally supplied evidence references/metadata, including an **unavailable** result without Observation; Phase 006-C must test if native Observation is truly necessary for an independently valuable suitability-only variant.
2. **Controlled Disclosure → Observation** may apply only to **observation-material release** variants, not protected nonresearch material. Phase 006-D must establish whether such a variant is in TreatFlow scope before promoting an edge.
3. **Delivery-specific inclusion:** a scoped product promise to deliver protected research materials entails both Research Provision and Controlled Disclosure participation under D-02, but a **feature-level combined action requirement is not automatically an unconditional concept dependency**. Test only after naming the variant and role.
4. **No universal edge cycle has been established.** Any prospective co-inclusion cycle requires role-based proof and must not conceal a Phase 004 independence defect.

## Representative guidance, not adopted subsets

- **Observation alone:** no currently accepted edge is violated, but this says nothing about full product-purpose sufficiency or adopted scope.
- **Observation + Stewardship:** contextually supports protected contribution recognition; it need not imply Revision.
- **Observation + Revision:** contextually supports factual history without assuming Stewardship.
- **Research Provision without Disclosure:** no-delivery suitability inquiry is not excluded by any currently accepted universal edge; its need for native Observation remains unresolved.
- **Disclosure without Research Provision:** protected nonresearch availability is not conceptually excluded; its inclusion as a TreatFlow variant remains unresolved.
- **Stewardship alone or Revision alone:** fails the **specific native-observation role** of the accepted conditional edges, but does not prove the generic concepts intrinsically incoherent.

**Historical disposition:** 006-C tested subset validity and 006-D selected adopted family scope. This document does not select a product tier, MVP or architecture.

**Limits:** actual minor/guardian consent, legal actor authority, protected release, retention, scientific methodology and operator feasibility must be established before real-world operation. Implementation is not authorized.

## 006-C subset, closure and cycle qualification

[006-C counterexample evidence](../../phases/006/006-c-treatflow-execution.md) sustains **S → O and V → O solely for TreatFlow-native observation contribution/correction roles**, not as universal independent-concept relationships. Within that context, a subset is edge-valid if S implies O and V implies O. Consequently closure({S}) = {S,O}, closure({V}) = {V,O}, closure({S,V}) = {S,V,O}, and closure({O}) = {O}. No accepted multi-hop extension or directed co-inclusion cycle exists.

**Discriminating cases:** {O}, {O,S}, {O,V}, {O,R}, {O,D} and {O,S,V,R,D} satisfy the presently accepted conditional edges, without thereby being approved products or safe operational configurations. {S} and {V} are **invalid for their named native-evidence role**, but can represent coherent generic responsibility/history behavior outside that context. {R} may support an external-evidence, no-delivery suitability inquiry only with legitimate available qualifiers; {D} may govern nonresearch material, though neither's TreatFlow product scope is established. An absence-only research result is not automatically a useful sustained application.

**Rejected apparent cycles:** O↔S, O↔V, S↔V and R↔D lack independent purpose justifications for mutual inclusion. Research Provision → Observation and Controlled Disclosure → Observation **remain undecided for adopted native scope** until 006-D; D-02's protected research-delivery participants are an action-level co-presence condition, not by themselves universal dependencies.

**Scope reminder:** edge-valid ≠ meaningful for full P-01–P-06 mandate ≠ adopted/in-scope ≠ verified protection. [006-D](../../phases/006/006-a-treatflow-execution.md) selects contextual roles and adopted variants; [006-E](../../phases/006/006-a-treatflow-execution.md) audits variant-specific composition. No product variant, implementation or data-use permission is authorized by this section.

## 006-D adopted native-family context — additional qualified edges

[006-D adopted conceptual scope](../project/concept-family-scope.md) selects an integrated **five-concept** TreatFlow-native observation/inquiry design. This is a project scope decision, not an assertion that all five are mathematically dependent on each other.

**Now supported for the adopted native roles:** `Research Provision → Observation` because R is intended to assess *TreatFlow-native reported evidence*, not independently imported external evidence; and `Controlled Disclosure → Observation` because D's chosen application role protects *observation-derived material*, not arbitrary nonresearch assets. These are **context-qualified extrinsic edges**, like S→O and V→O. Removing O defeats those adopted target roles, although generic Research Provision and Disclosure retain coherent independent applications elsewhere.

Thus in this selected native-evidence context: S, V, R or D included for its designated native role **implies O**. There is **no O→S/V/R/D dependency**; O remains independently valid and may be reported without any companion action. There is **no R→D or D→R universal inclusion edge**: D-02 protected research delivery involves both only *when that particular composed action is intended and its guards hold*. A no-delivery R assessment and D's independent protective denial remain coherent.

This paragraph supersedes earlier **pending** R→O/D→O questions **for the adopted native role only**; nonnative evidence/release roles are coherent but **outside adopted scope**. 006-E must test variant action-surface/guard coherence; 006-F retains independent exit authority. No real disclosure or data collection is thereby authorized.
