# TreatFlow — Provisional Concept Candidates

**Status: Retained for specification with carry-forwards at [002-F exit](../../phases/002/002-f-treatflow-exit-review.md); Phase 003-D conditionally specifies contribution control and factual revision; later specifications and exit review remain pending.** These working identities are *not final concepts*. Read the [purpose model](../purpose/purposes.md) and [002-E convergence rationale](../../phases/002/002-e-treatflow-execution.md). [Observation](observation.md) now has a provisional 003-C behavioral specification; other candidate specifications and cross-concept authority remain unresolved. Technical representation has not been decided. Phase 003 may revise, combine, split or reject any candidate. See [003-B identity challenge](../../phases/003/003-b-treatflow-execution.md) for behavioral stories and conditional decisions.

| Working candidate | Short semantic/behavioral hypothesis | Purpose association | Critical Phase 003/004 question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | An observer maintains a qualified account of monitored Halloween activity, potentially including event, interval and retrospective forms; explicitly differentiates watched quiet from unknown coverage | P-01, P-02, P-03 | Is exposure a coherent part of observation or a separate independently useful concept? |
| **Contribution stewardship** | A contributor can recognize and responsibly revisit the provenance and standing of their contributions without requiring public linkage to their identity | P-04, P-05 | Is this truly end-to-end user behavior or a fusion of participation identity, claim provenance and correction? |
| **Revision** | A contribution's factual errors or disputes can be addressed with understandable change history and responsibility | P-02, P-05, P-06 | Does revision qualify independently, or belong within Observation? How are contributor corrections unlike steward challenges? |
| **Controlled disclosure** | Information can be made available to an appropriate audience under conditions that limit avoidable harm, while respecting data consumers' research needs | P-03, P-04, P-06 | Is protected release independently coherent from research access and review? Who has authority? |
| **Research provision** | A recipient obtains a meaningfully scoped, interpretable body of observations accompanied by relevant measurement limitations, without prescribing the recipient's statistical conclusions | P-02, P-03, P-04 | Is this distinct from controlled disclosure, or merely its output? Can limitations remain intelligible without an in-product analysis suite? |

**003-C refinement:** Observation's current semantics are owned by [its specification](observation.md); the table below is candidate navigation, not competing specification authority.

**003-D status:** [Contribution Stewardship](contribution-stewardship.md) and [Revision](revision.md) now have *conditional* canonical behavioral specifications; their independent concept status is subject to 003-F/Phase 004. The list above is navigational and not competing specification authority.

**003-B disposition:** Observation proceeds to 003-C; Controlled disclosure proceeds to 003-E with its boundary under challenge. Contribution stewardship and Revision face conditional reframe-or-stop decisions in 003-D; Research provision faces a conditional merge-or-stop test in 003-E. None has a complete behavioral specification.

**Boundary watch, not yet a sixth retained candidate:** Participant trust/withdrawal and proportionate steward intervention could require independent behavior, but evidence does not yet justify promoting standalone mechanisms. Phase 003 must test whether these concerns can be fulfilled by candidates above and whether a distinct candidate must be reopened. Withdrawal of **future use** must never imply erasure of already-downloaded copies.

**Provisional coverage:** P-01 Observation; P-02 Observation/Revision/Research provision; P-03 Research provision/Controlled disclosure; P-04 Contribution stewardship/Controlled disclosure/Research provision; P-05 Contribution stewardship/Revision; P-06 Controlled disclosure/Revision with constrained stewardship. **P-03 and P-06 remain vulnerable** if evidence qualification or small-operator responsibilities disappear in detailed behavior.

**Not separately retained:** group, arrival, timestamp, confidence, location category, login, CSV, dashboard and Poisson computation are domain facts, qualifiers or possible means—not established independent behavioral concepts. The other divergent options and rejected/deferred seeds remain in [Phase 002 history](../../phases/002/).

No individual concept document is justified until identities survive behavioral specification. Implementation readiness **not ready**, execution **not started**, authorization **not yet**.
