# TreatFlow — Provisional Concept Candidates

**Status: Retained for specification with carry-forwards at [002-F exit](../../phases/002/002-f-treatflow-exit-review.md); Phase 003 exit PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD at [003-G](../../phases/003/003-g-treatflow-exit-review.md); Phase 004 exited PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD at [004-F](../../phases/004/004-f-treatflow-exit-review.md).** These five identities are the **current Phase 004 retained concept set**, subject to reopening if composition exposes a defect; they are not implementation architecture. Read the [purpose model](../purpose/purposes.md) and [002-E convergence rationale](../../phases/002/002-e-treatflow-execution.md). All five have distinct canonical specification owners linked below; Phase 004 established sufficient intrinsic modularity for Phase 005 entry, while cross-concept composition and real-world authority remain unresolved. Technical representation has not been decided. Future design may reopen or revise any concept with documented evidence. See [003-B identity challenge](../../phases/003/003-b-treatflow-execution.md) for behavioral stories and conditional decisions.

| Working candidate | Short semantic/behavioral hypothesis | Purpose association | Critical Phase 003/004 question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | An observer maintains a qualified account of monitored Halloween activity, potentially including event, interval and retrospective forms; explicitly differentiates watched quiet from unknown coverage | P-01, P-02, P-03 | Is exposure a coherent part of observation or a separate independently useful concept? |
| **Contribution stewardship** | A contributor can recognize and responsibly revisit the provenance and standing of their contributions without requiring public linkage to their identity | P-04, P-05 | Is this truly end-to-end user behavior or a fusion of participation identity, claim provenance and correction? |
| **Revision** | A contribution's factual errors or disputes can be addressed with understandable change history and responsibility | P-02, P-05, P-06 | Does revision qualify independently, or belong within Observation? How are contributor corrections unlike steward challenges? |
| **Controlled disclosure** | Information can be made available to an appropriate audience under conditions that limit avoidable harm, while respecting data consumers' research needs | P-03, P-04, P-06 | Is protected release independently coherent from research access and review? Who has authority? |
| **Research provision** | A recipient obtains a meaningfully scoped, interpretable body of observations accompanied by relevant measurement limitations, without prescribing the recipient's statistical conclusions | P-02, P-03, P-04 | Is this distinct from controlled disclosure, or merely its output? Can limitations remain intelligible without an in-product analysis suite? |

**004-B boundary decision:** [Observation](observation.md) retains monitoring exposure, evidence and reporting completeness as one coherent concept; **no standalone Exposure** is retained. [Rationale](../../phases/004/004-b-treatflow-execution.md). Further correction, ownership and research boundaries remain under audit.

**004-C boundary decision:** [Contribution Stewardship](contribution-stewardship.md) retains protected attribution/standing, but no longer owns generic requests to correct or withdraw other concepts' outputs. [Revision](revision.md) provisionally retains independent factual supersession history. Both face 004-E independent recheck. [Rationale](../../phases/004/004-c-treatflow-execution.md).

**004-D boundary decision:** [Controlled Disclosure](controlled-disclosure.md) provisionally retains independent prospective availability decisions, while [Research Provision](research-provision.md) is reframed around independently valuable *evidence fitness/qualification* rather than delivery artifacts; its survival remains conditional on 004-E. [Decision evidence](../../phases/004/004-d-treatflow-execution.md).

**004-E cross-candidate qualification:** All five working identities passed a provisional peer-removal, minimal-completeness and genericity challenge, **not** independent final exit. [004-E evidence](../../phases/004/004-e-treatflow-execution.md); 004-F may reopen or reject any result.
