# TreatFlow — Provisional Concept Candidates

**Current authority (through Phase 008-E):** The [Phase 006 adopted single five-concept family](../project/concept-family-scope.md) retains Observation, Contribution Stewardship, Revision, Controlled Disclosure and Research Provision; the [Phase 005 application action surface](../composition/application-action-surface.md) and C/D composition own guarded coordination, and [Phase 007 mapping](../mapping/integrated-action-language.md) governs user-facing distinctions. This file navigates concept owners and records qualified reuse dispositions, **not** a competing behavioral specification or an unresolved Phase 004 gate. Phase 002–004 questions and decisions below are chronological provenance; [008-E](../../phases/008/008-e-treatflow-execution.md) did not rewrite intrinsic behavior. No real-world collection/release authority is established.

| Working candidate | Short semantic/behavioral hypothesis | Purpose association | Critical Phase 003/004 question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | An observer maintains a qualified account of monitored Halloween activity, potentially including event, interval and retrospective forms; explicitly differentiates watched quiet from unknown coverage | P-01, P-02, P-03 | Is exposure a coherent part of observation or a separate independently useful concept? |
| **Contribution stewardship** | A contributor can recognize and responsibly revisit the provenance and standing of their contributions without requiring public linkage to their identity | P-04, P-05 | Is this truly end-to-end user behavior or a fusion of participation identity, claim provenance and correction? |
| **Revision** | A contribution's factual errors or disputes can be addressed with understandable change history and responsibility | P-02, P-05, P-06 | Does revision qualify independently, or belong within Observation? How are contributor corrections unlike steward challenges? |
| **Controlled disclosure** | Information can be made available to an appropriate audience under conditions that limit avoidable harm, while respecting data consumers' research needs | P-03, P-04, P-06 | Is protected release independently coherent from research access and review? Who has authority? |
| **Research provision** | A recipient obtains a meaningfully scoped, interpretable body of observations accompanied by relevant measurement limitations, without prescribing the recipient's statistical conclusions | P-02, P-03, P-04 | Is this distinct from controlled disclosure, or merely its output? Can limitations remain intelligible without an in-product analysis suite? |
