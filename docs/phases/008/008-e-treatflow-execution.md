| Concept | Catalog disposition | Transferable behavioral knowledge | Essential reuse constraints | Decision / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Revision** | **Reusable with constraints** | Independently protected factual assertion succession, non-destructive history, challenges and contested competing-successor currentness | No last-write-wins, silent overwrite, automatic authority, arbitrary fork selection or conflation with contribution-standing dispute | [concept owner](../../canonical/concepts/revision.md), [008-B](008-b-treatflow-execution.md) provenance revision lineage, [008-D](008-d-treatflow-execution.md) field-assertion correction |

**Classification rule:** “reusable candidate” or “reusable with constraints” records plausible transferability on the limited E1/E2/E3 evidence of 008-B, not a mature independently validated catalog concept. Reuse descriptions reference existing specifications; **intrinsic actions and invariants have one natural authority only**. A later reuse elsewhere requires independent purpose/behavior/authority testing.

## 3. Reusable knowledge not to mistake for copied implementation

Candidate reusable lessons are **semantic**. W3C provenance, DQV, DCAT and ODRL precedent references in [008-B](008-b-treatflow-execution.md) are partial conceptual comparisons, **not dependencies, compliance claims or adopted data formats**. Generalizing the concepts does not force an API, shared package, module hierarchy, persistence schema, concept catalog service or framework.

Phase 005 C-01–C-03 and D-01–D-03 compose the concepts *in TreatFlow's adopted role*; reuse of an isolated concept in another product does not inherit these synchronizations. Similarly Phase 006 S/V/R/D→O are context-qualified native inclusion edges, **not** universal conceptual dependencies.
