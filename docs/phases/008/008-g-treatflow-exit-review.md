## 6. Precise Phase 009 handoff — entry, focus, limits

Upon merge, **009-A — Integrity Audit Scope, Interference Surfaces, Whole-System Coverage & Subphase Planning** is next eligible. Phase 009's question is whether *each of the five concepts continues fulfilling its independent purpose when all five coexist under TreatFlow's actual application synchronizations, scope and representation*. Prior phases assessed local compatibility, but did **not** prove whole-system integrity.

Specific 009-A candidate investigation surfaces:
1. **Observer independence/participation:** S/V/R/D requirements must not impose unseen mandatory reporting friction, identity or authority transfer.
2. **Correction/currentness chains:** C-02 plus D-01/D-03 must not make fact correction ineffective or inaccurately assert currentness to downstream recipients.
3. **Protected standing versus factual authority:** C-01/C-03 and amendment/disclosure guards must not create cycles, privilege escalation or hidden adjudications.
4. **Research fitness versus protected dissemination:** D-02 denial must not erase an independently meaningful unsuitable/qualified research assessment, and protected release must not certify science.
5. **Temporal disclosure/recipient mental model:** changed prospective scope cannot retroactively rewrite old exposure or promise recipients notifications/copy recall.
6. **Mapping/language and reduced participation:** a combined experience must not reintroduce ownership, "published", "safe", "current" or fictitious product variants.
7. **Safety-policy gaps and small-operator feasibility:** record high-consequence external authority and privacy unknowns separately from proven semantic interference.

009-A must derive a **project-specific dependency-safe subphase plan**; do not pre-authorize its B–X sequence or substitute the 008 counterexample table for a whole-system investigation. If Phase 009 finds substantive inconsistency, repair upstream owners and repeat relevant checks before its exit; Phase 010 later tests broader adversarial scenarios.
