## 3. Purpose-quality hazards to challenge

- “Create an app,” “add authentication,” and “offer dataset download” describe possible mechanisms or requirements, not inherently the improvement experienced by a person.
- “Prove arrivals are Poisson” would bias data collection and analysis; a useful outcome may instead be honest rejection, uncertainty or an alternative explanation.
- A high submission count is not by itself meaningful success if observers are overburdened, exposure periods unknown, or the released data become identifying.
- Contributor convenience, research utility and household privacy can conflict; avoid assuming one optimizing measure resolves them.
- A voluntary contributor dataset does not automatically support inference about all neighborhoods or homes.
- Provisional adult-only, web-first and single-season boundaries from intake are candidate scope decisions, not human needs.

## 4. Approved substantive sequence

### 001-B — Actor Needs, Burdens and Participation Context

**Purpose:** develop need-focused accounts for contributors, analysts, operators and people who bear risks without using the app. **Inputs:** canonical intake, 000-B, 000-E, purpose-success contract. **Questions:** why record instead of merely remember; what attention/cost/privacy burdens exist; why would researchers trust or interpret reports; what would consent and meaningful avoidance mean for bystanders? **Evidence:** actor-need matrix, source status, contextual qualifiers and missing perspectives. **Canonical owner:** create compact `docs/canonical/purpose/purposes.md` when substantive work yields distinct purpose meaning; keep intake context in its current owner. **Exclude:** UI, access roles, session schema, policy mechanisms. **Completion:** important actors and affected nonusers each have explicit needs, burdens and uncertainties. **Handoff:** candidate separate needs to 001-C.

### 001-C — Product Purpose and Distinct Design Obligations

**Purpose:** translate B into coherent, sufficiently specific, evaluable, solution-neutral purposes. **Inputs:** 001-B and governing purpose contract. **Questions:** what change justifies TreatFlow, which improvements are independently necessary, where does scientific interpretability differ from disclosure protection, and what obligations can reject a misleading concept? **Evidence:** purpose statements with why/for whom/context/evaluability, alternatives rejected as feature-shaped, traceability. **Canonical:** refine the same purpose owner rather than one file per purpose. **Exclude:** final concept catalog, state/actions, predetermined Poisson acceptance criteria. **Completion:** purpose-level obligations distinguish valuable from merely requested functions. **Handoff:** purpose candidates to 001-D.

### 001-D — Context, Conflicts and Success Situations

**Purpose:** stress purpose candidates against Halloween observation realities and conflicting interests. **Inputs:** 001-B/C, 000-C/D risks. **Questions:** what constitutes success for a distracted observer, credible analyst, privacy-exposed household and manageable operator; when does one actor's success impair another's; what failure signals invalidate a nominally successful product? **Evidence:** concise before/after success situations (no UI scripts), tensions and misfit seeds; explicit assumptions and unresolved validations. **Canonical:** refine existing purpose owner with success/tension section if appropriate. **Exclude:** acceptance tests for an unchosen architecture, concept operational principles and Phase 010 exhaustive adversarial scenarios. **Completion:** success can be evaluated qualitatively without assuming an interface or statistical conclusion. **Handoff:** grounded model to 001-E.

### 001-E — Purpose Consolidation, Exit Review and Phase 002 Handoff

**Purpose:** review Phase 001 against [exit contract](exit-review-template.md). **Inputs:** B–D evidence, intake/purpose current authority, methodology. **Evidence:** coverage and purpose-quality audit, affected-party/tension traceability, cross-link/index integrity, assumption dispositions, recorded PASS / PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD / NOT READY TO EXIT and divergent concept-discovery handoff. **Canonical:** ensure singular current purpose owner and useful navigation. **Exclude:** performing Phase 002 or granting implementation permission.

**Dependencies:** B grounds purposes in affected parties; C decomposes those needs without solution lock; D checks contexts and evaluable success; E independently tests coherence and handoff.
