## Method

Challenge P-01–P-06 in grounded hypothetical Halloween situations. For each, distinguish a beneficial change from a superficial sign of completion or a harm borne by someone else. No interviews, field pilot, statistical tests, penetration tests or privacy compliance assessment took place. These are **provisional thought experiments**, not verified product performance.

## Scenario exploration

1. **Interrupted reporting:** a volunteer must attend to visitors and family rather than operate a reporting mechanism continuously. Meaningful success retains reasonable contribution and honest uncertainty; high event volume with lost observation fidelity is not sufficient. P-01/P-02.
2. **Single group, multiple visitors:** recording a group of five must not silently become five independent interarrival events. A future design should support intelligible measurement units without dictating an interface or schema now. P-02/P-03.
3. **Quiet versus unattended:** a monitored quiet interval is valuable evidence; no report during an unmonitored interval is unknown, not zero. P-01/P-02/P-03.
4. **After-the-fact correction:** an observer discovers a mistaken count or time; meaningful success preserves analytical honesty and reasonable accountability without exposing identity publicly. P-02/P-04/P-05.
5. **Non-Poisson or inconclusive finding:** analyst reaches a changing-rate, clustered, mixed, or uncertain conclusion and can state it with provenance and limitations. A predetermined 'Poisson confirmed' conclusion is failure. P-02/P-03.
6. **Nonparticipant household exposure:** fine geographic and time details, or repeat exports, identify a home even without a street address. This defeats protective purpose regardless of scientific utility. P-04/P-05/P-06.
7. **Public overgeneralization:** a self-selected sample yields a prominent regional comparison; meaningful success requires interpretation without claiming population representativeness. P-03/P-04.
8. **Peak operational demand:** misuse or withdrawal requests overwhelm a solo project; meaningful success requires proportionate trustworthy stewardship and clear scope, rather than uncontrolled scale. P-04/P-05/P-06.

The [canonical owner](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) contains the compact before/after and apparent-success checks. These scenarios are **purpose-level evaluations**, not software concept operational principles.

## Conflicting interests and unresolved judgment

The strongest asymmetry is between the **direct benefit to observers and researchers** and the **potential cost to household members or visitors who did not agree to participate**. More detail may aid arrival modeling yet increase location linkage, and protected accountability can conflict with pseudonymity. More reporting detail may improve scientific meaning while suppressing volunteers. Extra governance may protect people but undermine small-project sustainability.

Do not hide these conflicts behind a single adoption or satisfaction score. Future design must justify how the competing purposes are addressed; Phase 001 does not choose a release policy, role hierarchy, collection mode or security mechanism.
