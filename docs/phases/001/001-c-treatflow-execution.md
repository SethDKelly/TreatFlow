## Product purpose derivation

The initial request combined recording activity, geographic context, accounts, datasets and Poisson analysis. These specify potential solutions or research interests, not necessarily why functionality deserves to exist. The purpose formulation in the canonical owner instead focuses on **voluntary contribution to interpretable shared evidence under manageable burden and appropriate protection**. A finding that arrivals do not follow a Poisson process can still count as valuable research.

## Obligation decomposition and alternatives

Six provisional obligations have been distinguished: P-01 low-burden participation, P-02 intelligible observation, P-03 honest inference, P-04 nonparticipant/household protection, P-05 accountable correction, and P-06 manageable stewardship. Each has a distinct beneficiary, tension or failure mode, and a discriminating evaluation question in the canonical owner.

Alternatives explicitly rejected **as purpose statements** (but not necessarily as later design options):
- “Users must log in” — mechanism instead of trustworthy distinction, correction or protected access.
- “Provide a CSV export” — format instead of defensible research access and meaningful interpretation.
- “Record every child arrival timestamp” — overprescribes measurement and may conflate groups with people.
- “Show a Poisson dashboard” — UI and predetermined model rather than honest hypothesis exploration.
- “Maximize signups/rows” — activity metrics can improve even while quality and privacy deteriorate.
- “Make all data public” — unexamined access mechanism rather than balanced scientific and safety needs.

## Tradeoffs and independence checks

**P-01 versus P-02:** recording overhead may harm willingness to participate; effortless estimates may erase critical exposure/precision evidence.

**P-02/P-03 versus P-04:** finer temporal/spatial detail helps some analyses but may re-identify household routines.

**P-05 versus P-04:** internal accountability/correction does not justify persistent public pseudonyms or household-linked exports.

**P-06 versus P-01/P-03:** heavyweight operations could defeat the value of a small seasonal project, while under-governance can undermine trust.

These relationships are not resolved by forcing stakeholder consensus. Purpose alternatives and conflicts must remain available to divergent concept discovery.

## Purpose quality audit

- **Need-focused:** each obligation expresses a human/domain improvement rather than a feature.
- **Specific:** the six obligations can reject a design that meets one objective but violates another.
- **Evaluable:** each has a situation-based discriminating question, not a fabricated metric or threshold.
- **Context-aware:** time pressure, grouped visitors, incomplete exposure, sampling biases and linkage risks are acknowledged.
- **Evidence-aware:** all remain provisional sponsor-derived or inferential candidates.
- **Solution-neutral:** no concept catalog, workflow, schema, permissions model, stack or acceptance-test suite was selected.

## 001-D handoff

Test the canonical product purpose and P-01–P-06 against concrete **before/after success situations** involving a distracted observer, a meaningful quiet interval, grouped arrivals, a careful analyst, a nonparticipating household member, a correction/withdrawal request and a small operator. Distinguish success from apparent success, identify distributional effects and misfit seeds, and revise obligation boundaries if necessary. Reserve Phase 001-E for independent purpose/documentation consolidation and Phase 002 handoff.
