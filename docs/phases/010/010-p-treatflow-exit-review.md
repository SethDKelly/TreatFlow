# 010-P — Independent Phase 010 Exit, Documentation Audit & Phase 011 Eligibility

## Formal decision

**PHASE 010: PASS WITH EXPLICIT LIMITATIONS AND NON-OPERATIONAL BLOCKERS. PHASE 011-A: NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** This is a **document-based independent reassessment**, not review by an independent external human or empirical test. [010-O](010-o-treatflow-execution.md) is merged. The sponsor's explicit revised [primary purpose](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) is that a legitimate participant may **record and privately review qualified Halloween activity even without community reporting or research**. The current [Observation concept](../../canonical/concepts/observation.md), [family scope](../../canonical/project/concept-family-scope.md) and [Observation experience mapping](../../canonical/mapping/observation-representation.md) support this purpose without requiring S/V/R/D. This materially resolves the *conceptual central-purpose premise* underlying historical 010-H/J/L HOLD decisions; those decisions remain historically correct on their then-current purpose assumptions.

**This is NOT a PASS for empirical ease of use, lawful collection, minors' participation, privacy safety, actual research utility or publication.** Phase 011 may audit concept-design completeness; it may not authorize operational behavior or declare these missing controls implemented.

## 1. Exit criteria and changed baseline

- **Purpose and beneficiaries:** primary voluntary, intelligible private record/review; secondary optional responsibly qualified community summary; tertiary optional exploratory research. P-01–P-06 retain separate protection/accuracy obligations.
- **Scope:** the full five-concept vocabulary remains, but O-only report/review is an independently complete *experience*, not a separate product variant.
- **Concept/mapping ownership:** 010-N changes current purpose, O semantics and family scope; 010-O clarifies current Observation mapping. No new intrinsic concept/action, hidden mandatory research enrollment or speculative synchronization.
- **Historical consistency:** 010-A–G adversarial validation and 010-H–L HOLD records remain traceable. 010-M–O are a sponsor-approved, bounded change with targeted regressions, not retroactive edits to historical exit evidence.

## 3. Adversarial and integrity recheck

Reassessed [010-O's fifteen traces](010-o-treatflow-execution.md) for M01/M02/M03/M04/M06/M08/M09/M10/M11/M12 alongside [010-G's fourteen regressions](010-g-treatflow-execution.md). At document/specification level:

- Event time and report gesture cannot be silently equated; group encounters and number of people cannot be treated as independent events; an interval total alone is not monitoring completeness; missing periods are not observed zeros.
- Direct personal inspection is access within entitled scope, not publication, consent for research, or a claim that protected people approved source collection.
- An unresolved interrupted action is not known accepted/rejected and retries lack deduplication guarantees.
- S association, V correction, R qualification and D disclosure remain independent qualified actions, with C-01–C-03/D-01–D-03 protected bindings and no mandatory trigger from O.Report.
- Historical correction does not overwrite O evidence; D.SuspendFuture cannot recall earlier recipient copies; cumulative linkability and affected nonparticipants remain high-risk.
- Five independent native concepts, the 29-action surface, scope edges and prior Phase 009 integrity findings are not altered by the bounded purpose change.

**No confirmed remaining intrinsic conceptual contradiction or newly purpose-defeating primary recording/review misfit was demonstrated.** The claim is restricted to the documentary scenarios; usability, privacy and recovery are not empirically proven.
