# 010-M — Observation-First Purpose Clarification & Bounded Revalidation Plan

## Sponsor decision and start gate

**AUTHORIZED DESIGN INTENT:** a participant successfully records and reviews their own Halloween activity **even if no research provision or public/community report ever follows**. Quick capture and private review are the **primary purpose**; optional qualified community reporting is **secondary** and exploratory scientific inquiry **tertiary**. This is sponsor direction, not an assertion that end-user testing has been performed.

**Gate:** 010-L's HOLD remains historically valid under the former exit interpretation. This *new sponsor-approved purpose priority* is a material context decision that justifies a **bounded Phase 001 purpose amendment and dependent canonical-owner validation**, not automatic PASS, blanket reopening of every phase, or retroactive deletion of 010-H–L evidence. 011-A remains ineligible until independent exit re-review.

## Product promise to trace

**Primary success P0:** a legitimate participant, with an adequately safe personal participation context, can record and subsequently inspect their own qualified Halloween observations with minimal burden. This does not depend on S association, V registration, R qualification, D decision, or material availability to others.

**Illustrative but not binding UI shapes:**
- `NOW · TOTAL(4)`: four **people** reported for a claimed near-present event or report context; distinguish asserted occurrence time, entry time where known, group count if also declared, and precision/recollection. Do **not** equate four people with four independent arrivals.
- `6 PM–10 PM · TOTAL(83)`: 83 **people** reported within a bounded interval; this is an **interval-total claim**, not 83 independently timestamped arrivals. Reporting an interval does not by itself assert continuous attention during it; optional/uncertain coverage must remain distinct.
- Personal review: inspect original counts, units, time extents, provenance, coverage unknowns and legitimately scoped history. No requirement to join community reporting or research.

**Mode invariant:** in both entry styles, user benefit can be satisfied privately. Neither entry style is public consent, verified household identity, certified statistical evidence, published information or permission to collect identifiable children's data.

## Negative controls for reviewer

1. A participant must be able to inspect an unshared O-only observation without research enrollment.
2. Four people entered NOW must not become four independent Poisson arrival events.
3. A 6–10 PM total must not become an automatically watched four-hour interval, 83 timestamps, or exact encounter time.
4. Missing coverage must not become asserted zero.
5. Personal review must not expose the source to outsiders or imply permission for community reporting.
6. Closed Observation must not permit unguarded direct Report, while permitted historical Review and separate legitimate V corrections remain distinguishable.
7. Interrupted recording remains unresolved absent known effect; retry does not imply safe deduplication.
8. Even after conceptual PASS, D insufficient authority/protection still prevents actual data release and real participation remains subject to applicable safeguards.
