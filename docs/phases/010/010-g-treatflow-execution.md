## 2. Canonical repair and propagation

**Single current-owner edit:** [`docs/canonical/mapping/integrated-action-language.md`](../../canonical/mapping/integrated-action-language.md), new **Ambiguous outcome after an interrupted application action** subsection.

- Confirmed accepted means actual concept effect known with legitimate scope.
- Confirmed not effected requires evidence that no effect occurred; silence/timeout alone is insufficient.
- Unresolved means action effect is not established; do not imply success or failure.
- Repeated attempts must not be labeled deduplicated, exactly-once or safely reversible absent separate justified evidence.
- A presumed protected D.Decide or V.Amend completion cannot authorize downstream exposure or amendment.

**Semantic ownership:** user-visible application action interpretation belongs to Phase 007 mapping, not a new O/V intrinsic action. This clarification neither designs acknowledgments nor requires technical transaction, retry or idempotency mechanisms. If future empirical scenarios prove that users cannot distinguish duplicated factual assertions or recover low-burden submissions even with honest uncertain feedback, reopen the relevant 003/005 concept/composition owner rather than claiming the mapping edit resolves real usability.

**Propagation checked:** [O mapping](../../canonical/mapping/observation-representation.md) still describes feedback when effects occur; its positive feedback is now read subject to the cross-concept interrupted-outcome boundary. [Phase 005 action exposure](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md) and [C/D guards](../../canonical/composition/) are unchanged. The 29-action inventory, six guarded compositions, native five-concept scope, and prior phase evidence remain authoritative.
