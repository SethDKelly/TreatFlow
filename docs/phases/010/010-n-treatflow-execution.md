## Trace N01 — quick person count

**Observer intention:** `NOW · TOTAL(4 people)`. In a permitted observation context, O.Establish creates an account; O.Report adds one qualified EvidenceItem with unit **individual visitors/person count**, reported quantity **4**, approximate near-present occurrence extent and declared contemporaneous/recalled provenance and honest precision. It may have been one group, two groups, or unknown grouping; the reported count does not establish **four independently timed arrivals** or exact clock time. No AssertCoverage is inferred. O.Inspect permits the entitled observer to review the count, unit, claimed time and unknown monitoring coverage. O.Close and InspectAfterClose can preserve later private review without triggering S/V/R/D.

**Conceptual PASS:** Report/Inspect already suffice. Real-world speed and entitlement/participation safeguards are not empirically tested.

## Trace N02 — bounded interval total

**Observer intention:** `6 PM–10 PM · TOTAL(83 people)`. O.Report adds **one** measure asserting a count of 83 people **over that interval**, with declared retrospective/contemporaneous provenance and relevant uncertainty. No 83 individual timestamps, group event stream, or 4 hours of continuous observation is implied. Where watching coverage is unknown, it remains unknown; an explicit optional O.AssertCoverage could independently report observed, not observed or uncertain extents and reporting-completeness if justified. O.Inspect/InspectAfterClose returns the bounded total and qualifications to the entitled observer with no research/disclosure enrollment.

**Conceptual PASS:** time-bounded Measure/TimeExtent already support the claim. Actual summation method, potential partial counts and memory reliability remain a separate evidence question. A household total is not automatically a population arrival rate.

## Negative controls and downstream obligations

- Personal O-only reporting/review succeeds without S.Associate, V.EstablishSubject, R.RequestEvidence, D.Propose or public release.
- Report submission gesture and claimed event time remain distinct; `NOW` is shorthand, not a guarantee of event-time precision.
- Neither mode infers continuous monitoring from an interval, positive coverage from activity, or a zero for missing events.
- Four persons are not four independent Poisson events; 83 persons over four hours are not 83 event timestamps.
- A private personal review neither discloses contributor identity to others nor establishes lawful source collection, visitor tracking or guardian authority.
- Close prevents direct new O.Report but entitled InspectAfterClose and separately guarded legitimate V correction remain meaningful.
- Ambiguous interrupted Report outcome still uses [confirmed accepted / confirmed not effected / unresolved](../../canonical/mapping/integrated-action-language.md); no claimed automatic deduplication.
- Protected community/research use continues to require actual separately qualified and legitimate R/D conditions, with no real data release authorization.
