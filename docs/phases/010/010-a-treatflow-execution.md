## 1. Validation ensemble and legitimate bounds

**In scope:** voluntary household observer during Halloween activity; research question about time/volume and possible Poisson fit; contributor/guardian/other household member/visitor as affected parties; claimant, factual amender, qualified researcher, protected decision-maker and small volunteer operator as **conceptual positions whose real-world legitimacy is unresolved**. Include short seasonal peak, interrupted monitoring, retrospective corrections, delayed disputes, after-season research, historical recipients and repeat releases where unlinkability may degrade.

**Application scope:** precisely one integrated O/S/V/R/D native family. O-only reporting, unassociated items, rejected/inconclusive R and withheld D are experience states within that family, **not** separately marketed variants. No anonymous-public raw dataset, automatic visitor tracking, identity inference, forced credentials, recipient-copy recall, certified Poisson analysis or production release is adopted.

**Environment/pressure:** door interruptions, partial attention, mixed group/person measures, sparse/missing observations, imprecise clock recollection, family co-location, strategic falsification, correlated observations, uneven geography, time-lagged decisions, uncertain protected actor eligibility, volunteer operator scarcity, recipients outside direct control. Offline/device/network events matter only if they imply distinct *user-visible conceptual* partial/uncertain/duplicate outcomes; no runtime recovery design.

## 4. Scenario method, coverage discipline and evidence

For each substantive validation scenario record: **ID, P-purpose, actual context and affected non-initiators, initial legitimate state, actor/authority assumptions, trigger/mistake/incentive, ordered actions and optional C/D composition, known versus unknown information, current expected behavior, adverse user-visible result, fitness disposition, semantic owner if repair is needed, and revalidation impact**. Distinguish representative success, tolerated limitation, **confirmed conceptual misfit**, blocked high-consequence unknown, and downstream engineering concern. Use contradictory/counterfactual versions and delayed/partial states where they materially expose a different misfit.

Coverage must include all five concepts and P-01–P-06; positives and negatives; contributor and nonparticipant viewpoints; missing/recalled/incorrect evidence; disputes and delegated/unknown authorities; prospective versus historical disclosure; research insufficiency; strategic use; peak-season workload; and conceptual recovery/non-reversibility. Stop elaborating scenarios when they no longer expose a different purpose, actor, guard, time or failure mechanism; **scenario counts are not exit evidence**. Phase 010-G will check coverage, independent critique and remaining risks.

Evidence grades: **document-trace supported**, **hypothetical misfit/counterexample**, **external-policy prerequisite unverified**, **empirical evidence unavailable**. Do not label prospective user tests executed or assert practical enforcement. A discovered intrinsic/composition/variant/mapping contradiction requires reopening 001/003/004/005/006/007/008/009 as its natural owner, publishing one current correction, and rerunning both success and adversarial regressions.

## 5. Dependency-safe project-specific validation phases

**010-B — Representative Observation, Evidence Quality & Research Success/Failure Scenarios.** Execute S01/S03 with M03/M04: watched quiet versus gap, group/person units, imprecise and self-selected reports, scientifically unfavorable results. Gate: positive success *and* honest negative inference documented.

**010-C — Contributor Identity, Mistakes, Contested Claims & Recovery Semantics.** Execute S02 with M02/M04/M05/M09/M11: shared households, claimant challenge, duplicate or mistaken contribution, guarded correction after close, incomplete/pending resolution. Gate: no forged authority, silent truth-selection or misleading recovery.

**010-D — Protected Disclosure, Minors, Nonparticipant Safety & Irreversible Exposure.** Execute S04 and only hypothetical S05 with M01/M02/M06: audience/material risk, linkability, repeated release, missing approval basis, prior copies, recipient harm. Gate: withheld path always meaningful; no blanket consent or false recall.

**010-E — Longitudinal Research, Stale Knowledge, Mapping & Scientific Misfit.** Integrate S01–S05 under M03/M07/M08/M12: private V visibility, as-of qualifications, unsupported Poisson claim, delayed recipient interpretation and combined state vocabulary. Gate: new results do not manufacture scientific validity/recipient notification or reveal protected history.

**010-F — Strategic Misuse, Operational Limits, Exceptions & Conceptual Recovery.** Test M09–M12 across actors: fabricated observations, exploitation of contested standing, denial/timeout/interruption conceptual consequences, season-peak operator scarcity and strategic disclosures. Gate: defined bounded semantics for unresolved/declined results without invented engineering processes.

**010-G — Adjudication, Upstream Repair, Regression & Risk Reconciliation.** Consolidate verified misfits, route corrections to natural 001/003–009 owners, propagate to canonical knowledge and retest representative success and negative cases; stop if confirmed high-consequence conceptual defects remain. Gate: no uncorrected blocking misfit, evidence-calibrated residual register.

**010-H — Independent Scenario-Validation Exit, Documentation Audit & Phase 011 Handoff.** Independently challenge the substantive evidence, scenario diversity, current-vs-history ownership and accepted limits. Gate: explicit Phase 011 methodology-closure eligibility, or deny exit and reopen repairs.

**Sequence:** B/C establish observation and actor baseline → D addresses exposed persons and irreversible consequences → E integrates longitudinal inference/representation → F applies misuse/resource pressure → G adjudicates and revalidates → H independently decides exit. Reopen earlier owners immediately on confirmed contradictions rather than letting them persist until G.
