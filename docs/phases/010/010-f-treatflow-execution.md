## 2. Conceptual recovery stress findings

**Unknown action result is not failed action.** O.Report, V.Amend or D.Decide may have occurred even when the actor did not observe acknowledgment. The current concepts specify completed semantic state but **not attempted-versus-committed outcome knowledge**. Blindly retrying may introduce another EvidenceItem or Revision branch; falsely claiming success may lead to wrong scientific conclusions or premature exposure. Where entitled, Inspect/InspectHistory/InspectDecision may clarify committed state, but an entitled view and item identity may still be insufficient to prove that a particular interrupted attempt was accepted.

**Misfit hypothesis for adjudication (F09–F12):** a low-burden reporting purpose and accountable correction may need an explicit *application-level epistemic contract* for what an actor may conclude after an ambiguous attempt, including how to distinguish confirmed accepted, confirmed rejected and **unresolved**, without choosing a queue, network protocol, retry key or database mechanism. Whether this is already adequately covered by [Phase 007 action availability mapping](../../canonical/mapping/integrated-action-language.md), or requires a narrowly scoped canonical clarification, is **OPEN / HIGH PRIORITY for 010-G**.

**Strategic dispute load is not harmless:** protected legitimacy guards prevent automatic wrongful reassignment but may leave valid contributors under sustained contest. The design cannot assume an always-available adjudicator, indefinite safe pending state or unbounded operator resources. Phase 010-G must decide if current meaning of “contested, pending, unresolved” suffices conceptually or whether a user-visible promise is unsatisfied. No invented timeout approvals or default claimant powers.

## 3. Protected disclosure and downstream consequences

A malicious actor can combine sparse event times with public posts or repeat innocuous disclosures. D's guard conceptually requires defensible consideration of material/audience/linkability, but **no validated protective criteria, actor eligibility, enforcement or recipient control exist**. A positive release is consequently not authorized by this paper test. D.SuspendFuture cannot recall prior copies and a contributor standing decision cannot substitute for household/visitor permission.

When adversarial release risk competes with research usefulness, **legitimate unavailable/unsuitable evidence is a meaningful result**; it does not prove the proposed product can deliver scientifically useful and safe released material. Preserve P-03/P-04 research/protection tradeoff as a **material product-fit evidence gap**.
