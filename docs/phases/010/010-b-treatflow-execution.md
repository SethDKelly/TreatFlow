## 1. Representative success — S01 observed group and coverage

**Context and affected parties:** voluntary Halloween observer, interpreter/researcher, household members and visiting children. O.Establish creates an open observational account without S/V registration. Observer reports `AssertCoverage` as watched 18:00–18:20, one group arrival at approximately 18:07 with **group encounters = 1** and **visitors in group = 5**, an explicit not-watched gap 18:20–18:35, and a recalled estimate in 18:35–18:50 with uncertain attention/time precision. O.Inspect exposes the distinct measure, coverage and provenance qualifications to entitled interpreter; O.Close prevents subsequent O.Report.

**Expected and traced outcome:** P-01 participation remains low-burden and P-02 keeps attended quiet, missing watching and uncertain recall semantically separate. The absence of a report does **not** prove zero arrivals or complete recording even while watching. One five-person group is not five statistically independent group-arrival events. **SUCCESS AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL**, not evidence of actual volunteer burden or data quality.

## 2. Representative success — S03 scientifically unfavorable inquiry without release

**Context:** a qualified researcher considers whether TreatFlow-native observations could support a time-varying arrival-rate or Poisson-process inquiry. The researcher requests a specific evidence scope via R.RequestEvidence. R.PrepareScope distinguishes available coverage, event unit, reported timestamps and missingness; R.QualifyProvision records volunteer self-selection, uncertain watching and possibly contested source standing if legitimately known; R.InspectProvision communicates **limited, unsuitable or unavailable** suitability where necessary. R.Provide never follows merely because the request exists, and D authorization is not inferred.

**Expected and traced outcome:** R yields a meaningful scientific *assessment* with no source delivery or certified model result. P-03 is served by permitting the answer “the information does not support this inference.” **SUCCESS AT SPECIFICATION LEVEL** conditional on actually known/entitled evidence. No Poisson statistical analysis was performed.

## 4. Misfit and scientific boundary adjudication

**Confirmed conceptual defect in S01/S03/M03/M04 tests: none.** The present concept distinctions can represent ordinary observation, absent coverage, contradictory data and an unfavorable research qualification without inventing observations. The essential limitation is **scientific**: voluntary self-selection, reporting omissions and clock imprecision can make actual inference unsuitable even with internally coherent state. Treating a scientifically unfavorable conclusion as valid behavior is not a defect, but presenting it as population certainty would be.

**Potential contextual misfit reserved:** real volunteers might be unable to express coverage or provenance without disproportionate burden, or analysts might conflate persons/groups despite the conceptual distinctions. This is **unverified user/domain fit**, not evidence of successful usability; 010-E and 010-F should pressure representations and human constraints. No substantive Phase 003/005/006/007 reentry is justified solely by these traced results. Any later scenario proving an unavoidable contradiction must reopen its natural semantic owner immediately.
