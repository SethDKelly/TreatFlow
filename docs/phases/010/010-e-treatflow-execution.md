## 1. End-to-end success and temporal challenge

**Positive baseline:** one observer legitimately reports one group of five in a qualified interval; O.Close preserves the source record. A later researcher R.RequestEvidence/PrepareScope/QualifyProvision evaluates stated units, exposure, recall and selection limitations **as of that qualification**. If this supports only an unfavorable/inconclusive inference, R.InspectProvision communicates that result without D delivery. Separately, an entitled factual amender may add V successor “four,” retaining the original assertion and rationale; a newly qualified inquiry may consider that standing *only where entitled and known*. An appropriately authorized D decision (if such real authority/policy existed) would have to match the actual material and audience, not simply inherit prior permission.

**Time points:** t0 O report → t1 R assessment of known evidence → t2 hypothetical D protected decision/exposure → t3 V correction/challenge → t4 new R inquiry / changed D audience → t5 future restriction. No t3 action rewrites the already-held t1 interpretation or t2 copy. Sequence is conceptual and does **not** specify automated reassessment, notification, retention, or release.

## 3. Key interpretive pressure: private history versus credible fitness

An R qualification cannot treat *inaccessible* V material as known, but also must not declare evidence **fully current** solely because no challenge is visible to its interpreter. Canonical D-01 and Phase 007 mappings already permit “unknown/limited/unavailable.” This is the safest current conceptual resolution; it is **not** proof that participants will understand the distinction between “no visible challenge” and “no challenge exists.” 

**Provisional fit disposition:** no semantic repair justified solely from these cases; mark the combined representation of *knowledge boundary, as-of standing and scientific limitations* for targeted misfit/adversarial comprehension challenge in 010-F and adjudication in 010-G. A demonstrated user-facing contradiction would reopen the Phase 007 mapping owner rather than broadening access to V protected history.

## 4. Longitudinal scientific and protective tradeoff

At one extreme, reducing granularity enough to protect sparse households may eliminate event-time exposure required for a particular Poisson inference. At the other, event-level precision and home-level geography may produce unacceptable linkability. The two independent concepts support the honest outcome **research unsuitable and material withheld**; but that possible outcome alone is *not* evidence that TreatFlow's aspirational public-research purpose can be materially fulfilled in its proposed context.

**Material product-value evidence gap:** establish later, with appropriate privacy and scientific expertise, whether any responsibly qualified, appropriately available evidence supports useful inquiry. Do not solve that question by assuming a coarse release is anonymous, a data sample is representative, or scientific interest overrides D. Route genuine P-03/P-04 conflict to original purpose/scope owners if 010-G/H finds no plausible satisfactory path. Actual collection and release remain unauthorized.
