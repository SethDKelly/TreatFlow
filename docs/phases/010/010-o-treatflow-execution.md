### Dependency and conceptual integrity

- **O-only complete:** `Establish → Report → Inspect` (or `Close → InspectAfterClose`) is an independently successful primary experience inside the adopted five-concept family; the absence of optional S/V/R/D never disables an otherwise legitimate report.
- **C-01–C-03:** item/party/subject identities remain distinct; standing does not confer amendment/disclosure rights; protected revision after close does not rewrite O.
- **D-01–D-03:** R as-of fitness does not grant release, D prospective restriction is independently necessary, and prior exposure cannot be recalled conceptually.
- **Phase 006:** contextual S/V/R/D→O inclusion edges are not mandatory participation chains or automatic actions.
- **Phase 008 familiarity:** “NOW,” “total,” “owner,” “approved,” “published” and “revoked” must not imply stronger semantics than the concept warrants.
- **Phase 009 integrity:** five retained independent concepts, 29 intrinsic actions and existing guarded C/D compositions remain unchanged; no new missing synchronization established.

**Evidence grading:** these are **specification-level counterfactual traces**, not actual task-time measurements, participant research, scientific sample tests, legal evaluation, privacy risk certification, or working UI tests.

## Residual risks and gate handoff

**Core primary-purpose design:** supported at conceptual specification level by valid simple count and bounded total, qualified personal Inspect, and independence from publication/research. **User effort and accessibility** have not been measured; do not label empirical validation complete.

**Community/research protection:** no real collection from minors or affected persons and no actual release authorized without independent eligibility, lawful/privacy safeguards, cumulative linkability and authorized decisions. Positive scientific research value and safe distribution remain **unproven**; these must be explicit downstream validation/pre-operation blockers, not silently declared fulfilled.

**Practical recovery/operator:** interrupted action comprehension, duplicate attempts, adversarial use and staffed adjudication remain untested. The canonical Phase 007 uncertainty rule is a semantic boundary, not an implementation guarantee.
