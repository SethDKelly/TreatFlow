## 1. Representative success S02 — accountable protected correction

An observer creates a qualified `O.Report` of one group of five visitors, without mandatory S enrollment. If independently justified, C-01 maps that specific `EvidenceItem` to a separate `S.Item` and legitimate `S.Party`, allowing a protected `S.Recognize` of responsibility. After `O.Close`, an independently authorized actor uses C-02 to bind the qualified assertion to a `V.Subject` and `V.Amend` to produce a successor count of four with reason. `V.InspectHistory` distinguishes the original five, new four, legitimate actor scope and any contested currentness; O remains closed and its earlier assertion is not overwritten.

**Result:** **S02 success under stated legitimacy guards**. `S.Associate` does not automatically run on every report, S standing is not an edit entitlement, and O close is not a ban on separately protected factual correction. Real verification of Party/amender legitimacy remains missing; the conceptual trace does not authorize any particular claimant.

## 3. Partial completion, recovery and authority boundaries

- **Mistaken factual content:** `O.Qualify` may add qualifiers while O is open but must not silently replace a contradictory factual assertion. Where legitimate, V establishes a new historical successor. `O.Close` does not imply irrevocability of eligible Revision, and amendment does not imply reversal of a previous recipient copy.
- **Mistaken target/Party:** `S.Associate` requires justified binding and claimant legitimacy; `S.ContestStanding` makes disputed responsibility intelligible. Neither public identity disclosure nor edit permission is a correction shortcut.
- **Unknown request outcome:** conceptual truth cannot be inferred from an interrupted gesture. The present concept specs distinguish the state that exists if an action completed, but **do not establish an application-level acknowledgment/retry/idempotency promise**. Label this an *unresolved fit question*, not a false assertion that a new retry concept is already required; 010-F must test the observer's ability to recover without fabricating or duplicating evidence.
- **Strategic misuse:** assertion provenance ≠ credibility; an authenticated or apparently legitimate contributor ≠ truthful event. Qualification and protected decisions cannot silently become proof of observation.
- **Irreversible consequences:** relinquishing standing, amending facts and restricting future availability are different outcomes. None guarantees erasure of distributed material.

## 4. Adjudication and correction routing

**No confirmed intrinsic O/S/V purpose-defeating flaw** was established under their current guards. The materially open issues are (a) evidence sufficient to establish real Party/amender/guardian/adjudicator standing; (b) duplicate/ambiguous-outcome recovery in a low-burden seasonal context; (c) malicious claims and overwhelmed dispute resolution; and (d) comprehensible distinctions between standing, amendment and erasure.

If 010-F demonstrates an unavoidable conceptual failure for ambiguous outcomes, reopen the *natural owner*: `Observation` behavior under Phase 003 if an assertion identity/status is underspecified; Phase 005 application action exposure if composed acknowledgment/attempt meaning is essential; Phase 007 mapping if the user cannot distinguish unknown/completed outcomes. Do not invent API retry, durable queue, login or workflow architecture in Phase 010. Confirmed authority deficits route to the appropriate protected semantics owner; concrete eligibility and guardian policy remains **not established** and blocks real operation.
