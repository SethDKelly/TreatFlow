# TreatFlow — 010-H Independent Scenario-Validation Exit, Documentation Audit & Phase 011 Handoff

## Formal independent decision

**HOLD — PHASE 010 NOT YET EXITED. Phase 011-A NOT ELIGIBLE.** Phase 010's *planned scenario work* (010-A–G) is complete and [010-G](010-g-treatflow-execution.md) is merged on main, but the [Phase 010 exit contract](exit-review-template.md) requires defensible representative-purpose fitness and disposition of material misfits, **not simply a completed scenario inventory**. Current documentary scenarios support honest observational reporting, guarded historical correction, meaningful scientifically unfavorable inquiries and protective refusal. They do **not yet establish** a feasible positive pathway that preserves both useful research and nonparticipant protection, or the real authority and evidence prerequisites for any actual collection/disclosure. These are central to [P-03/P-04](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md). Neither a speculative “possible approval if everything is justified” nor universal withholding establishes positive purpose fitness.

**Disposition:** Keep 010-H as the independent reviewed HOLD decision, not a fabricated PASS or Phase 011 start authorization. Targeted additional 010-I evidence/fitness closure is required before repeating the exit decision. If relevant new evidence demonstrates that the promised protected research value cannot be fulfilled in the adopted scope, **reopen Phase 001 purpose and Phase 006 scope** rather than declaring a well-guarded but purposeless delivery path sufficient. No actual information collection, disclosure or implementation is authorized.

This review is **independent in method**—rechecking current owners and cross-phase evidence against the exit template, rather than adopting the 010-G recommendation as an exit verdict. It is **not independently performed by a separate reviewer, external scientist, privacy professional or user-testing panel**. Do not represent it as external verification.
