## 3. Independent adversarial exit judgment

### Specificity

Observation's event and monitoring behaviors answer one coherent measurement purpose. Contribution Stewardship's generic correction/withdrawal request behavior was removed; it now concerns responsibility standing only. Revision concerns **factual** successor history, not contributor attribution. Controlled Disclosure controls prospective sharing, while Research Provision qualifies potential evidence; a restriction decision and an unsuitable-research conclusion are separable benefits. **Finding: no material known purpose overload remaining.**

### Minimal completeness

For each canonical operational principle, the necessary internal actions/state are described and the core result can be understood without a peer concept: observe/inspect, recognize/contest responsibility, amend/inspect history, approve/withhold/suspend prospective exposure, or qualify an inquiry as meaningful or unsuitable. A real actor's eligibility/permission is a necessary **external legitimacy guard**, not silently satisfied by these conceptual descriptions. Removing the cited core actions would destroy the relevant purpose; optional behavior is not artificially required. **Finding: adequate for Phase 005 entry.**

### Independence and genericity

Peer-removal and identity-permutation probes from 004-B–E were challenged against the final owner documents. Identity-only objects remain abstract; substantive distinctions—visitor versus group, monitoring completeness, contested responsibility versus contested truth, exposure history, research missingness/selection—remain typed and meaningful. No canonical owner requires another peer's private state or defines a built-in action chain. **Finding: sufficient conceptual independence; interaction may still be required at application level.**

### Changed-concept re-specification

Contribution Stewardship was rewritten in place to include purpose, OP, abstract state, guards, actions, lifecycle and protected standing invariants after removal of generic requests. Revision's factual-only challenge was clarified. Research Provision's suitability-first OP and no-delivery outcome were clarified without creating a dataset-access component. Observation and Controlled Disclosure were refined at their original owners. **Finding: Phase 003-quality behavioral meaning preserved after factoring.**

### Alternative/counterexample credibility

Explicitly compared: event-only vs period-only vs split Exposure; Observation-embedded vs independent responsibility/correction histories; correction vs attribution disputes; disclosure-only vs research-only vs combined vs artifact-oriented offering; peer-removal and action-deletion negative controls. Rejected options remain in dated phase records and can be reconsidered with contrary evidence. **Finding: more than confirmatory review.**

## 4. Purpose and safety integrity

- **P-01:** bounded/recalled reporting permitted without mandatory full monitoring; user effort remains empirically untested.
- **P-02:** group vs visitor semantics, watched zero vs absent coverage, retrospective uncertainty and contested revisions preserved.
- **P-03:** nonrepresentative selection and uncertain exposure remain expressible; Poisson fit can be rejected or deemed inconclusive; no inferential algorithm certified.
- **P-04:** no default public source/household identity; withhold when protection basis insufficient; minors/nonparticipant risk still requires later policy and validation.
- **P-05:** contributor standing does not equal fact truth or unilateral amendment authority.
- **P-06:** conceptual control/qualification does not presume enterprise staffing; real operator workload is not validated.

No concept grants real consent, age eligibility, lawful access, effective anonymization, permanent deletion, safe disclosure or an acceptable study sample simply by being well factored.

## 6. Phase 005 composition handoff — hypotheses, not synchronization definitions

The [Phase 005 definition](../005/phase-definition.md) and [005-A start gate](../005/005-a-start-gate.md) own workstream planning. Candidate questions:

1. **Observation ↔ Revision:** when a source assertion is corrected, which application interpretation is current? Preserve earlier evidence, contested branches and post-close distinctions without modifying either intrinsic concept definition.
2. **Stewardship ↔ Revision:** what standing/authority check permits amendment while distinguishing a factual challenge from responsibility contest? Never infer edit entitlement from household identity.
3. **Revision ↔ Research Provision:** how are known supersession and unresolved factual disputes reflected in later inquiry qualification? Do not imply automatic retroactive correction of already-supplied results.
4. **Controlled Disclosure ↔ Research Provision:** how do separately determined availability and suitability influence whether evidence is provided? Permission cannot establish scientific fitness, and suitability cannot confer permission.
5. **Evidence/material changes ↔ Controlled Disclosure:** when does prior availability permission need reassessment? Preserve prospective-only suspension and historical exposure.
6. **Application action surface:** which intrinsic actions will be available directly, only through application composition or intentionally not exposed? A valid concept action need not become a user-facing application action.
7. **Over-/under-synchronization:** avoid coupling all reporting to registration or release, and avoid promising corrected/restricted evidence without conceptual relationships supporting that promise.
8. **Chaining and safeguards:** qualify possible chained action consequences, guard compatibility, failure/denial semantics, and protection of third parties *at the conceptual layer*.
9. **Phase 006 handoff:** actual concept inclusion and extrinsic dependencies; **Phase 008:** broader familiarity/generalization beyond the necessary Phase 004 corrections.

Nothing above authorizes or prescribes any concrete synchronization, action trigger, API, data flow, execution sequence or technical dependency.

## 7. Explicit carry-forwards and reopening rules

**Non-blocking for Phase 005 conceptual entry:** application composition choices, intrinsic action exposure, possible product inclusion/extrinsic dependence, broader concept familiarity and independently validated user value. During Phase 005, if no safe/complete semantics can be composed, reopen 004 or 003 rather than hiding new intrinsic behavior in a synchronization.

**Blocking before real-world operation:** participant/guardian consent and minors' eligibility, visitor/household privacy and linkage risk, genuine release authority and safeguards, storage/retention/withdrawal/deletion policy, scientifically defensible sampling/inference, and a viable small-operator response model. A future Phase 005 PASS will not remove those prelaunch gates.
