## 1. Adversarial cross-candidate tests

| Concept | Peer-removal / falsification example | Purpose-critical behavior | Independence finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Observation](../../canonical/concepts/observation.md) | Contributor reports one group without permission system, revision or research; complete monitored zero separately requires suitable coverage and completeness | Establish, Report, AssertCoverage, Qualify, Inspect, Close | **Retain** integrated event/coverage behavior, no Exposure split; source identity is intrinsic assertion origin, not an imported account concept |
| [Contribution Stewardship](../../canonical/concepts/contribution-stewardship.md) | Two people contest responsibility for a generic contributed item, even if item cannot be amended or disclosed | Associate, Recognize, ContestStanding, DetermineStanding, Relinquish | **Retain provisionally** bounded responsibility association; does not own identity verification, generic request handling or factual amendment |
| [Revision](../../canonical/concepts/revision.md) | A generic qualified claim has a corrected successor and conflicting branches without Observation or Stewardship existing | EstablishSubject, Amend, InspectHistory, Challenge, ResolveChallenge, MarkSuperseded | **Retain provisionally** factual succession history; distinct from ownership dispute and future-use withdrawal |
| [Controlled Disclosure](../../canonical/concepts/controlled-disclosure.md) | Operator withholds abstract material from an audience due to potential harm, without inquiry-scoping or statistical analysis | Propose, Assess, Decide, MakeAvailable, SuspendFuture, InspectDecision | **Retain** prospective permission/exposure history independent of research fitness |
| [Research Provision](../../canonical/concepts/research-provision.md) | Investigator requests suitability of hypothetically available evidence; returns *unsuitable* due to missing monitored exposure, with no delivery authorization | RequestEvidence, PrepareScope, QualifyProvision, InspectProvision | **Retain provisionally** independent inquiry fitness finding; Provide is optional, requires separately established availability and is not necessary for characteristic OP |

**Negative control:** A generated file with caveats and no inquiry-fitness behavior would fail as Research Provision. A `Party → Item` relation with no way to contest or inspect would fail Stewardship. An overwrite-only action without history would fail Revision. This review did not protect names from rejection merely because the documentation exists.

## 2. Specificity and minimal completeness

- **Observation:** different actions report events, monitored extents and completeness *for one purpose*; remove coverage and observed zero cannot be interpreted. No forced continuous reporting or manufactured unknown-as-zero.
- **Stewardship:** one purpose is responsibility standing; correction/release requests removed in 004-C. A dispute and rationale can be interpreted without an externally executed correction.
- **Revision:** one purpose is interpretable successor history. A factual challenge does not mean ownership challenge, and acceptance cannot fabricate a missing corrected assertion.
- **Disclosure:** one purpose is controlled prospective availability. Proposal, authority decision and real exposure are separate facts. An insufficient safety basis cannot produce an approved release.
- **Research Provision:** one purpose is scoped evidence fitness assessment. Its independent outcome can be unsuitable, inconclusive or meaningfully qualified **without delivery**. Provisioning a file is not its defining purpose.

No obvious missing intrinsic action was found that blocks Phase 004-F review. **Authority to use a concept's protected action is still an unresolved policy/participation obligation**; asserting an abstract legitimate actor does not establish a real authorization or consent regime.

## 3. Genericity and type-permutation audit

| Type family | Permutation result | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| ObservationId / Observer source tokens | Renaming opaque tokens preserves assertion meaning if source association is preserved | Abstract identities accepted; the observation itself still owns assertion origin semantics |
| Party / Item / Subject / Material / Audience / EvidenceReference | Bijections preserving equality/standing and applicable rights leave intrinsic behavioral transitions unchanged | Keep independently defined generic identity domains; do **not** import peer state/types |
| Group encounter vs individual visitor | Renaming units into each other changes count/interarrival interpretation | **Do not erase** measure-unit semantics |
| Watched coverage, completeness and time precision | Substitution changes whether an inferred observed zero is legitimate | **Do not generalize away** monitoring and time semantics |
| Authorized versus contested standing; factual supersession; prospective suspension | Semantic classifications affect legal conceptual transitions | **Do not treat** these as arbitrary identity tokens |
| Inquiry scope and research caveats | Replacing missingness, volunteer selection or disputes with unqualified output changes scientific meaning | **Preserve** qualifications and unsuitable/inconclusive outcomes |

Concept independence does **not** mean that generic identities lack any later product relationships. Such relationships are Phase 005 composition hypotheses, Phase 006 inclusion questions and later rights/policy decisions, not peer-private types inside concept state.

## 4. Authority and temporal contradiction probes

| Challenge | Required semantics | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Two source claims at same house | No automatic shared contributor identity/control | Observation source and independent Stewardship standing clarified |
| Standing contest versus factual challenge | A legitimate association decision does not rewrite factual assertion | Concept boundaries kept separate |
| Closed Observation versus later correction | Direct reporting closed; independently authorized historical correction may still be meaningful | Revision/Observation remain separate; no automatic write across boundary |
| Multiple successors to same assertion | No silent current-truth selection | Revision retains contested branches and explicit qualified standing |
| Changed evidence after a disclosure | Prior approval cannot silently cover materially different content | Disclosure scope/decision invariants retained |
| Withdrawal after dissemination | Future availability may be restricted; previous external copies not guaranteed recalled | Disclosure's prospective/history split retained |
| Unqualified or self-selected sample | A research inquiry can be marked unsuitable without release | Research Provision's no-delivery OP and qualifications strengthened |
| Insufficient household/minor protection | Abstract policy guard is not evidence actual release is safe | Real-data release and collection remain **blocked** pending later review |

## 5. Corrective re-specification and current authority

Corrections were applied in place under the five natural canonical owners rather than creating another set of phase-specific concept documents:

1. [Observation](../../canonical/concepts/observation.md) clarifies source is the abstract origin associated with an account, not a responsibility/adjudication claim.
2. [Stewardship](../../canonical/concepts/contribution-stewardship.md) clarifies independent claim/contest resolution cannot authorize edits or future release.
3. [Revision](../../canonical/concepts/revision.md) clarifies correction's independently valuable result and distinct factual authority from standing.
4. [Controlled Disclosure](../../canonical/concepts/controlled-disclosure.md) clarifies external assessment bases are inputs/guards, not another concept's imported state.
5. [Research Provision](../../canonical/concepts/research-provision.md) clarifies no-delivery qualification as a first-class terminal outcome and distinguishes externally available evidence metadata from authorization.

All retain purpose, OP, abstract identity/state, conceptual actions with guards/effects, invariants, historical/temporal semantics, intrinsic authority constraints and deliberate under-specification. These clarifications do not introduce new application purposes or executable mechanisms. Historic 003 material remains evidence; 004-B–D factual boundary decisions remain intact.
