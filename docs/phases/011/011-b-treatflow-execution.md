- **D 6/6:** propose, assess, decide, conditionally make available, suspend future, inspect decision → P-04/P-06, optional P-03 availability; no past-copy recall.

**Current inventory outcome:** no obvious orphan action, concept or currently justified C-01–C-03/D-01–D-03 composition. This is a documentation-level completeness claim; **011-C must independently examine individual behavior/guard integrity** rather than accepting the inventory as proof. Contextual S/V/R/D→O is inclusion for TreatFlow-native purposes, **never mandatory action or intrinsic O dependency**. The chosen five-concept family remains one product, not an O-only product variant.

**B03 / CRITICAL operational — collection/release authority:** P-04 guards express legitimate decision requirements but **do not establish real guardian/household consent, eligibility, privacy feasibility or operator policy**; no actual collection or disclosure authorized. **011-E explicit hard no-go**, not a reason to grant operational readiness.

Interrupted reports, wrong counts and overloaded adjudication are unresolved practical fit. **011-C classify** and **011-E carry forward**; reopen O/mapping only on demonstrated semantic defect.
