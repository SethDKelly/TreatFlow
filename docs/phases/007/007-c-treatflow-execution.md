## Mapping and entitlement tests

1. **Reporter with no association:** Observation.Report remains intelligible and independently available. No associated Party or revisable Subject is manufactured.
2. **Two contributors in one household:** private Item–Party standing is separately established if legitimate. Same address or device cannot infer shared Party, amendment rights or public authorship.
3. **Contested attribution:** entitled view separates active, contested and relinquished standing; `ContestStanding` changes standing only. `DetermineStanding` cannot silently change factual evidence.
4. **Corrected group encounter:** an authorized five-to-four `Amend` creates a successor Assertion with its predecessor/history; it neither overwrites Observation's original report nor creates a new direct Report.
5. **Closed observation corrected later:** Closed forbids Observation additions, but an independently eligible registered Revision.Subject may still be amended; statuses must not be conflated.
6. **Factual challenge only:** unresolved challenge remains distinct from changed asserted value; no false amendment or permission transfer.
7. **Conflicting successor branches:** competing currentness remains contested, with no arbitrary newest-wins rule.
8. **Relinquishment:** contribution standing may end without deleting evidence, retracting a recipient's copy or suspending disclosure.
9. **Insufficient protected authority:** denied or unavailable protected participation yields no hidden successful action; an observer's valid original report survives.
10. **Public reader:** no implicit access to private Party identity, factual history or protected source assertions.

## Complete action-surface disposition

**Contribution Stewardship (5 actions):** `Associate` is internal/optional, `Recognize`, `ContestStanding` and `Relinquish` are direct protected, and `DetermineStanding` is protected with legitimate decision basis. Each has actor/target, availability, successful semantic result and nonavailability representation.

**Revision (6 actions):** `EstablishSubject` and `MarkSuperseded` remain internal/conditional; `Amend` protected/composed; `InspectHistory` direct qualified; `Challenge` direct protected; `ResolveChallenge` protected. Denied/unknown authority, contested factual currentness, original/current/historical version distinctions and outcomes are accounted for.

**C-01–C-03:** Only qualified `EvidenceItem → Item` or `EvidenceItem → Subject/Assertion` correspondence can support optional recognition/protected correction; neither creates automatic reactive actions. Standing contests and factual challenges retain distinct protected adjudication. No cross-concept identity equivalence is assumed.

## Adversarial misrepresentation results

**Rejected:** shared household as common owner; reporter as automatic amender; contested standing as verified authorship; `DetermineStanding` as a factual correction; `Challenge` as a completed amendment; destructive correction; latest-wins in forked history; closed Observation reopened through Revision; relinquishment as deletion; protected actions universally available to the public.

**Residuals:** actual Party legitimacy, standing adjudicator, amender and factual resolver entitlement remain unresolved upstream protected-policy obligations. No public identity exposure or universal right to inspect history can be inferred. The existence of a historical correction does not guarantee notice to earlier Research Provision recipients, alteration of delivered documents or retroactive disclosure recall.
