## 1. Core mapping decision

A requester can obtain an intelligible **unsuitable/inconclusive/unavailable assessment** without becoming a recipient of private material. Conversely, a protected authority can consider or refuse future availability without certifying that an inquiry is scientifically adequate. An actual composed research delivery requires independent research qualification and material/audience disclosure guards (D-02), not either judgment alone.

Version-aware research interpretation (D-01) uses *legitimately known* standing, not global privileged inspection of all factual histories. Changed future material/audience cannot borrow obsolete approval (D-03). Past releases remain historical and no automatic recipient notification or repaired copy is promised.

## 2. Action and query coverage

**Five Research Provision actions** mapped: `RequestEvidence` direct qualified, `PrepareScope` and `QualifyProvision` internal, `InspectProvision` direct qualified, `Provide` internal/composed.

**Six Controlled Disclosure actions** mapped: `Propose`, `Assess`, `Decide`, `SuspendFuture` protected; `MakeAvailable` internal/composed; `InspectDecision` direct protected.

Mapping identifies scoped state questions for requester, entitled researcher, assessor, authorized decision-maker, prospective recipient, previous recipient and affected nonparticipant. For each, distinguish qualified action availability, success, denial, insufficient evidence and history, without claiming public identity or data rights.

## 3. Stress cases and non-exposure

| Counterexample | Correct interpretation |
|---|---|
| Self-selected, missing-exposure observations | Research suitability may be limited/unsuitable, never automatic Poisson confirmation |
| Favorable R but D declined or not justified | Research qualification stands; **no protected delivery** |
| D approved for unrelated/changed audience, R lacks fitness | No valid composed delivery or research quality claim |
| A scientific inquiry is only requested | No entitlement to underlying raw records |
| Material changed after an approved disclosure | Further release under new scope needs independently valid authorization |
| Old material was delivered, then factual claim corrected | Prior exposure and qualification-time standing remain; no automatic recipient notice/copy recall |
| Protected actor or safe-release criteria not established | No affirmative disclosure or guessed authority |
| Public reader requests household-linked source material | No universal public `Provide`, `MakeAvailable` or unrestricted inspection |
