## 2. Integrative state and language tests

| State/path | Misleading mental model rejected | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Watched quiet vs absent reporting | “Zero events” inferred from empty log | Affirmative attention claim needed for observed silence |
| One group, five visitors | Five independent arrival events | Group encounter and person count differentiated |
| Recalled time | Exact contemporaneous event clock | Asserted time precision and provenance visible |
| Reporter vs steward vs factual amender | “Owner” controls evidence and release | Source, responsibility and amendment authority separate |
| Contested standing vs factual challenge | One generic “disputed” status | Party–Item dispute and Assertion challenge distinguished |
| Revised but closed observation | Correction reopens original recording | Separate historical Revision; Observer still closed |
| Research qualified vs disclosure approved | “Ready to download” from research fitness | Independent evidence suitability and audience/material permission |
| Approved decision vs delivered material | Approval means access occurred | Decision is not ExposureRecord |
| Future suspended vs historically exposed | “Revoked” erases old copies | Suspension prospective only |
| Unknown/ineligible vs unavailable | One red error for all | Distinguish missing evidence, unsuitability, authorization and lifecycle state |

This is a **semantic terminology audit**, not a final dictionary of interface strings. Terms are qualified by actor, scope, as-of standing and consequence wherever material.

## 3. Context-specific action mapping

**Observer:** a valid report is direct qualified, low-burden and independent of other four concepts' participation. A closed account blocks new direct observation reporting, not a separately legitimate Revision amendment.

**Entitled contributor or claimant:** protected contribution recognition/contest/relinquishment concerns responsibility standing only. The actor must not be shown as a verified public identity or factual amender absent independent legitimacy.

**Entitled amender/challenger/reviewer:** different factual amendments/challenges and determination actions retain target and outcome. Unresolved competing successor branches do not show an unqualified unique current version.

**Research requester:** a request and non-delivery qualification can yield limited/unsuitable/unavailable findings without access to raw material. `InspectProvision` is not unconditional `Provide`.

**Protected disclosure authority and recipient:** valid material/audience risk decisions are independent of scientific fitness. Protected research delivery requires both D-02 guards, and prospective-only D-03 changes never erase historical exposure.

**Affected nonparticipant:** no raw timestamp, household/visitor identity or protected standing is exposed based merely on an observation/report source or a research request.

## 4. Semantic accessibility and structural audit

Faithful views must preserve distinctions under distracted contribution, low-attention reading and noncolor/alternate perceptual modes. Missingness, uncertain time, measurements, disputed status, protected availability and historical exposure must be comprehensible without relying solely on color, position or animation. Consequential protected-action target, eligible authority, prospectively effective scope and denied outcomes must remain meaningful in context.

This does **not** specify mobile/desktop layouts, accessibility code, control design, framework, route hierarchy or display mechanism. It specifies what an eventual user-facing representation cannot obscure.

## 5. Synchronization and scope integrity

- C-01–C-03: reporting optional association; protected correction history distinct from responsibility disputes; no forced chain.
- D-01–D-03: legitimate known currentness affects prospective interpretation; dual-guard release and change-in-scope consideration; no autonomous permission/release or historical notice.
- Phase 006: one adopted integrated O/S/V/R/D family, not separately released O-only/O+R product editions.

**No confirmed upstream defect:** none of these bounded obligations requires a new intrinsic action or undocumented synchronization. New guarantees of automatic requalification, recipient notifications, source-level public data or identity equivalence must be returned to Phase 005/003/006 rather than invented as representation.
