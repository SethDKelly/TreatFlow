## 1. Mapping problem and boundary

The design asks how a willing Halloween observer, legitimately affected contributor, entitled researcher, protected disclosure decision-maker and other affected parties can **understand what the application knows, what an action means, who is eligible, what occurred and what remains uncertain or unavailable**. Concept state and semantic action guards are authoritative; linguistic/physical presentation is subordinate. An application may combine views where beneficial but must preserve who owns factual state and what changes.

Phase 007 can require distinctions in naming, grouping, ordering, persistent contextual visibility and consequences *only to prevent conceptual confusion*. Phase 008 will examine broader familiarity. No representation may manufacture automatic events or permissions absent in 005.

## 3. Viewpoint and state-query inventory for planning

| Perspective | Required semantic question / consequence | Risk if absent |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary observer, potentially distracted | Was time actively watched? What was counted: groups or persons? Was report retrospective and how uncertain? Is the account closed? | Quiet time misread as zero, visitors as independent arrivals, uncertainty lost |
| Source/contributor with legitimate standing | Which individual item is associated with me, what standing is contested and who can decide it? | Household/source mistaken for verified public ownership |
| Legitimate factual challenger/amender | Which original assertion, successor and contested history am I acting on? Can I amend after account closure? | Correction looks like overwrite or reopening; unauthorized amender implied |
| Research inquirer | What inquiry scope, observation exposure, unit, missingness, challenge state and suitability decision are justified? | Bias/missingness disguised as statistically valid Poisson evidence |
| Disclosure proposer/assessor/decision-maker | What material, audience and future scope is being evaluated? What authority, risk and historical exposures exist? | Consent implied from scientific fitness, prior copy falsely recalled |
| Entitled recipient/public audience | Is anything actually qualified and permitted for this audience, and which historical version did it represent? | Suitability conflated with access, restricted raw material exposed |
| Nonreporting household member, child/guardian or other affected party | What is not being publicly implied or exposed about me, and whose legitimate authority is required? | Invisible privacy burden and false notion that observer controls everyone's data |
| Small seasonal operator | Which protected judgments remain pending, unsupported or unperformed, without an assumed automatic process? | False operational assurance and disproportionate handling promises |

This identifies perspectives, **not** implementation identities, authentication roles, screen assignments or guaranteed public inspection.

## 4. High-risk representation counterexamples

1. **A quiet hour appears as a count of zero** even though the observer had stopped watching. Require watched/unknown differentiation.
2. **A group of five appears as five arrival events.** Preserve observational unit separately from visitor count.
3. **A late report appears exact and contemporaneous.** Distinguish reported event time, submission time and uncertainty.
4. **“My observations” falsely means verified authorship and edit rights.** Source, legitimate Stewardship standing and Revision amendment authority are not interchangeable.
5. **A corrected group count silently replaces original evidence.** Original/successor/challenge and qualified currentness must remain intelligible.
6. **A correction after Close appears to reopen the observation.** Keep Observation closure and independent Revision history distinct.
7. **“Research ready” looks like “publicly downloadable.”** Evidence fitness never substitutes for valid D audience/material authorization.
8. **“Access revoked” appears to erase previous recipient copies.** SuspendFuture is prospective; historical exposure is retained.
9. **R/D actions appear immediately available to every viewer.** Internal/protected actions do not become public UI commands.
10. **All five concepts appear compulsory on each report.** Presence in adopted family must not impose optional cross-concept actions.
11. **A rejected/inconclusive inquiry is presented as a failed technical action.** Distinguish substantive unsuitable/unknown from invalid invocation or missing permission.
12. **Anonymous/coarse location is described as guaranteed household-safe.** Avoid unverified protection claims.

If a required view answer cannot be derived from existing conceptual state/action semantics, **reopen 003/004/005/006 as appropriate**, not invent a convenience field.

### 007-C — Contribution Standing, Factual Revision, Authority & Historical Representation

### 007-D — Research Suitability, Controlled Disclosure & Protected Recipient Understanding
**Purpose:** map R and D state independently, inquiry caveats, suitability unknown/unsuitable, prospective material/audience decisions, composed D-02 and historical exposure. **Inputs:** R/D specs, D rules, earlier mapping. **Evidence:** scoped query/feedback mapping and scientific-value-versus-release-authority counterexamples. **Gate:** no protected release, false representativeness or copy recall. **Next:** E.

### 007-E — Integrated Application Actions, Availability, Language & Structural Integrity
**Purpose:** reconcile direct/protected/internal/composed action mapping, semantic vocabulary, status distinctions, authority-based availability, physical/structural constraints, accessibility and no-delivery/reduced-participation states. **Inputs:** 007-B–D, adopted scope and 005 action inventory. **Evidence:** application-intention matrix across actors and states; linguistic collision audit; structural/accessibility constraints independent of UI design. **Gate:** all material action exposure mapped, no hidden automatic actions or variant-as-edition drift. **Next:** F.

### 007-F — Experience Counterexamples, Misfit/Reentry Review & Mapping Completeness
**Purpose:** challenge the assembled mapping with Phase 001 scenarios and adversarial cases, test stale/historical/unknown statuses and protected impacts; reopen upstream semantics if genuinely missing. **Inputs:** 007-B–E, P-01–P-06. **Evidence:** falsification record, precise nonblocking carry-forwards or repair obligations. **Gate:** no unresolved material misrepresentation hidden as future frontend work. **Next:** G.

### 007-G — Documentation Audit, Exit Review & Phase 008 Handoff
**Purpose:** independent exit against [Phase 007 exit template](exit-review-template.md), canonical ownership/OKF/index integrity, action-state coverage and future familiarity review preparation. **Inputs:** B–F and current canonical mapping owners. **Evidence:** explicit exit outcome, remaining limits, 008-A entry. **Gate:** Phase 007 complete only after independent evidence review, never UI implementation.

**Sequence:** B establishes observed evidence representation → C attribution/history → D inquiry/protection → E integrated action/language/structural audit → F falsification/reentry → G independent exit. No user-interface build or architecture work is authorized.
