## 3. Cross-owner fidelity and protected effects

**Concept identity:** Observation.Observer, Stewardship.Party, Revision.Subject/Assertion, ResearchProvision.EvidenceReference and Disclosure.Material/Audience are different abstract identities. A combined user experience cannot infer correspondence without justified C/D bindings.

**Actions:** the original Phase 005 inventory is 7 Observation, 5 Stewardship, 6 Revision, 6 Disclosure, 5 Research = **29**. Direct qualified ≠ unrestricted; protected actions need independent entitlement; internal/composed participants are not standalone public commands. Nothing in Phase 007 introduces a universal `Report → Associate`, `Report → EstablishSubject`, `Amend → research update`, or `Decide → Provide` action chain.

**Time/history:** watched quiet ≠ no reporting; a group of five ≠ five independently timed arrivals; recalled event time ≠ exact fact; a closed Observation ≠ a prohibition on independently legitimate Revision; competing successors do not automatically define unique truth; a qualified research assessment is not a disclosure decision; disclosure approval is not historical exposure, and prospective suspension does not recall past recipient copies.

**Authority and affected parties:** no public contributor identity, household authority, guardian consent, amendment entitlement, scientific readiness or reidentification-safe released data is inferred. A participant's capacity to submit a report does not make nonparticipants' information freely distributable.
