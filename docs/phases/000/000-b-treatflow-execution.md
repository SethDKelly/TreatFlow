## Opportunity and contextual interpretation

The sponsor's objective is a fun, community-generated dataset describing how many trick-or-treaters arrive and when, with broad location classifications. This supports voluntary reporting and exploration of whether arrival patterns resemble a Poisson process. This **does not establish** statistical validity, product-market demand, completeness of observations, or any existing empirical shortage. The opportunity is to produce interpretable records rather than a predetermined statistically significant result.

Evidence classes:
- **Stakeholder assertions:** desired reporting system, unique contributor identification, timestamped counts, geographic context, safe data exports, and fun scientific analysis.
- **Contextual inference:** time-limited Halloween activity and multiple possible observer/analyst audiences follow from the premise but have not been independently validated.
- **Hypothesis:** some arrival series might fit a Poisson model; group arrivals, changing rates, event clustering and volunteer-selection effects are plausible challenges, not established findings.
- **Proposals:** web/mobile delivery, sign-in design, group logging, dashboards, export representations or access policy. None are frozen as solutions.
- **Potential constraints requiring qualification:** protection against disclosure of households and potentially minors, reliable attribution of contributions and fair disclosure of data limitations. No jurisdiction-specific legal determination is asserted.

## Outcomes and potential failure

- Easy voluntary contribution: failure if data entry requires more attention than users can reasonably give on Halloween.
- Useful research data: failure if timestamps are ambiguous, groups are mistaken for individuals, reporting windows are unknown, or zero activity is mistaken for nonparticipation.
- Responsible access: failure if downloads reveal household identity or sensitive presence/absence patterns through quasi-identifiers.
- Interpretable findings: failure if analysts mistake self-selected, incomplete measurements for a representative experiment or assume Poisson truth.
- Sustainable small project: failure if implementation or governance demands outgrow the hobby/research scope.

These are *outcome candidates* for Phase 001 scrutiny, not finalized product success metrics or promised features.

## Alternatives and tensions preserved

Low friction versus strong contributor identity; fine temporal precision versus location confidentiality; public accessibility versus misuse prevention; self-report simplicity versus study-quality observation; visual engagement versus misleading claims of statistical certainty.
