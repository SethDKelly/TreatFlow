## 3. Resolved semantic omissions versus deliberate openness

**Resolved within this PR:**
1. An observed zero is not inferred from an empty report list or attention alone; a declared measure and suitable reporting completeness are needed.
2. Overlapping contradictory coverage claims stay contested for interpretation.
3. Accepted control requests are distinguishable from externally fulfilled actions.
4. Multiple incompatible revision successors stay contested, without arbitrary current-truth selection; challenges are not amendments.
5. A closed Observation remains closed to direct additions, while independent legitimate correction is not automatically impossible.
6. Insufficient disclosure safety basis never implies approval or certification of anonymity.
7. Future disclosure suspension cannot recall earlier copies, and changed material does not inherit authorization silently.
8. Research provision exposes known sampling, coverage, unit, time, uncertainty and dispute limitations; insufficient data may be marked unsuitable rather than falsely complete.

**Deliberately unspecified:** database persistence, algorithms, schedules, APIs, UI, event provenance storage, precision values, format for released materials, statistical tests, authentication technologies and exact disclosure risk thresholds.

**Actual open issues with named destinations:** institutional/household consent and children's protection; rights to resolve contested identity, factual amendments and disclosures; independent concept boundaries; whether provenance needs its own purpose; actual operational capacity. These cannot be claimed solved via behavioral document language.

## 4. Cross-candidate authority and independence review

- A contributor association establishes *conceptual responsibility*, not ownership of Observation's private state or a public identity.
- Revision history describes claim standing, not an automatic Observer mutation. Any future realization relationship is a **Phase 005 synchronization question** after factoring, not a Phase 003 hidden workflow.
- A disclosure decision authorizes only scoped prospective exposure; Research Provision cannot grant permission. A scientifically useful inquiry cannot force release.
- A changed research provision or source correction may require prospective disclosure reconsideration; **no implicit orchestration** is designed here.
- Independent purpose concerns remain for **Contribution Stewardship**, **Revision** and **Research Provision**. Their specification now provides concrete state/actions for a Phase 004 merge/split/rejection audit; provisional retention is not a promise of five final concepts.
- Intrinsic authority is expressed through legitimate contributor, amender, reviewer or distributor guards; **which parties qualify and how** remains a later authority/policy question, not automatically an approved implementation.
