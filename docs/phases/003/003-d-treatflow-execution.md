## Cross-concept separations and refusal cases

- **Source ≠ household:** two observers at one residence are not automatically the same contributor or entitled to each other's claims.
- **Responsibility ≠ factual truth:** being recognized as a steward is not evidence an arrival occurred.
- **Request ≠ fulfillment:** an accepted or pending control request does not by itself change Observation, disclosure or third-party copies.
- **Factual amendment ≠ consent withdrawal:** correct an asserted quantity without pretending to withdraw already consumed results. Future-use restrictions belong to later protected disclosure/authority work.
- **Challenge ≠ amendment:** an analyst may query trustworthiness without permission to overwrite original information.
- **Historical intelligibility ≠ permanent retention:** keeping versions distinguishable when revisions are in scope does not decide data retention law, disclosure or guaranteed restoration.
- **No silent cross-concept action:** neither concept imports Observation's private abstract types/state or assumes synchronized mutation; interactions await later design phases.

## Specification adequacy and explicit blockers for 003-F

Each provisional specification supplies purpose, characteristic OP, abstract domains, minimal state, conceptual actions with guards/effects, history/invariants and under-specification. **But conceptual precision is not yet sufficient for final Phase 003 exit in three areas:** (1) accepted control request effects versus unavailable external action; (2) revision branching and definition of current assertion; (3) intrinsic authority and post-closure correction semantics. These must be specified, simplified by merge/reframe, or explicitly found nonessential in 003-F; do not carry accidental omissions into Phase 004.

**Possible consolidation:** If Revision is an action/history of Observation, incorporate its needed semantics into [Observation](../../canonical/concepts/observation.md) and retire the separate current specification. If Stewardship cannot offer meaningful behavior beyond association/authorization, similarly reframe and retire. 003-F and Phase 004 may revisit this decision without violating Phase 002's five-candidate history.
