## Identity and OP assessments

### 1. Observation — proceed to 003-C (P-01, P-02, P-03)

**Purpose hypothesis:** Let an observer convey what activity was actually witnessed, within intelligible observation coverage and uncertainty, with tolerable effort.

**Characteristic OP story:** During a Halloween evening, a volunteer observes a group of five arriving together. They contribute an account of that encounter as a group of visitors, with the time as precisely known. A quiet interval watched throughout can be understood as observed silence; a later period when the volunteer was away is unknown, not zero. Someone interpreting the account can tell observed event units, periods watched, and uncertainty without silently inferring missing events.

**Identity challenge:** This is recognizable observer-facing behavior rather than a static arrival entity. Compare an event diary, period accounting and bounded claims; does a separately independent Exposure concept earn its own end-to-end user purpose, or would it split one coherent observation promise? **003-C must explicitly falsify this OP if adequate coverage cannot be expressed without excessive reporting effort.**

### 2. Contribution stewardship — conditionally proceed to 003-D (P-04, P-05)

**Purpose hypothesis:** Let a contributor recognize and exercise legitimate continuing responsibility for their own contributions without turning their person or residence into public research identity.

**Characteristic OP story:** Two volunteers at the same house contribute overlapping observations. Each can recognize which contribution they made and later seek an authorized correction or change of future use. A researcher receives qualified evidence without automatically acquiring the volunteers' identifying details. Source responsibility is distinguishable from household identity or the truth of the reported encounter.

**Identity challenge:** *Stewardship* risks obscuring unrelated identity, participation, provenance and authorization concerns. At 003-D, demonstrate an independent characteristic change or decision belonging here rather than simply associating a login with Observation/Revision. If no defensible autonomous OP and minimal memory/actions emerge, **reframe into contribution responsibility, absorb into another concept, or return to discovery**—do not invent a generic account platform. Authorization specifics remain open.

### 3. Revision — conditionally proceed to 003-D (P-02, P-05, P-06)

**Purpose hypothesis:** Make a previously asserted contribution's correction intelligible and accountable without obscuring original claims or exposing identities.

**Characteristic OP story:** A contributor realizes that the reported group size of five should have been four. An authorized amendment distinguishes the earlier and current assertions so later interpretation need not silently rely on the obsolete count. Separately, a recipient may challenge credibility without acquiring permission to rewrite the contributor's account. A request to stop *future* disclosure is not described as a factual correction and cannot recall copies already obtained by others.

**Identity challenge:** Revision may be a natural action/lifecycle within Observation or a reusable independent history-bearing concept. At 003-D, test its own user-facing purpose, abstract memory and conceptual actions independently of the object revised; if it is merely an edit operation, **absorb rather than force a concept**. Third-party challenge and withdrawal are distinct authority questions, not preapproved extra concepts.

### 4. Controlled disclosure — proceed to 003-E with separation test (P-03, P-04, P-06)

**Purpose hypothesis:** Enable information to become available under accountable, limited exposure conditions that take nonparticipants' interests seriously.

**Characteristic OP story:** A small number of geographically close participating homes produce fine-timed observations. Before information is made available to a research audience, its potential to expose recognizable household routines is considered; availability may be restricted, transformed or declined. A subsequent recipient cannot infer from mere publication that the sample is representative or safe to cross-link. A change in future availability does not retrospectively erase material already distributed.

**Identity challenge:** A recognizable protected-sharing lifecycle may be independently meaningful, but its boundary with Research provision is uncertain. At 003-E, test whether disclosure owns availability/withholding decisions while provision owns interpretable consumption, or whether that is a single coherent user purpose. **No safe anonymization threshold, permission protocol or disclosure policy is set here.**

### 5. Research provision — conditionally proceed to 003-E (P-02, P-03, P-04)

**Purpose hypothesis:** Let a research recipient obtain evidence with the scope and qualifications necessary to avoid misinterpreting observation units, exposure and sampling.

**Characteristic OP story:** A researcher seeks Halloween arrival evidence. The material available for their inquiry carries intelligible distinctions among group encounters, individual counts, monitored quiet, gaps and approximate times, together with qualifications about voluntary sampling. They may conclude a Poisson model is implausible or the evidence is inconclusive; the service does not certify a favored model.

**Identity challenge:** If the candidate merely names a collection of rows delivered by Controlled disclosure, it has no independent end-to-end behavior. At 003-E, test whether provision enables a distinct recognizable selection/qualification/use lifecycle, or must **merge with disclosure or be treated as output**. Keeping analysis external remains an explicit valid alternative.

## Cross-candidate falsification matrix

| Challenging situation | What would defeat a proposed identity |
|---|---|
| Monitored quiet versus absent observer | Observation cannot express this difference without hidden inference or impossible work |
| Two contributors at one address | Contribution stewardship conflates person, site, and research-visible provenance |
| Correcting count and withdrawing future use | Revision pretends a factual amendment is consent withdrawal, or promises third-party recall |
| Rare house in coarse region | Controlled disclosure presumes coarse location always provides anonymity |
| Inconclusive or non-Poisson finding | Research provision silently presents biased measurements as representative or imposes Poisson confirmation |
| Solo seasonal operator | Combined identities demand unbounded grants, review queues or manual interventions |

**Distributional protection:** people who do not participate, including visitors and children, cannot be treated as consenting merely because contributors find a behavior useful.
