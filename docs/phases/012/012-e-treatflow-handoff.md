## Authority separation: three independent decisions

1. **Concept closure:** Phase 011-F closed the design with bounded limitations and may justify pre-implementation preparation. It is not execution authorization.
2. **Engineering execution:** After independent Phase 012-F exit, a distinct downstream lifecycle may *consider* representation, architecture and implementation. It must separately authorize actual coding and its environment, security and test access. Nothing in Phase 012 confers that authority.
3. **Real-person collection, processing or disclosure:** Separately authorized operational decisions are required even if a downstream engineering program is approved. For R-P02/R-P03 there is an express no-go until the appropriate safeguards and competent decisions exist. Anonymous/synthetic non-production scenarios do not by themselves validate real-person operations.

No agent may self-approve a protected decision. Agent rule files, PR merge authority or possession of infrastructure credentials do not satisfy human/organizational approvals.

## Architecture-neutral acceptance and non-goals

Downstream engineering must account for:
- typed observation unit (people vs groups), asserted temporal precision, monitored/unknown coverage, bounded interval totals and private inspect/after-close rights;
- conditional standing, contest, factual supersession and dispute history; independent R evidence suitability and D decision/exposure records;
- protected actor authority, affected nonparticipant safeguards, voluntary participation and scoped recipient decisions;
- distinctions between attempted, confirmed, rejected and unresolved actions; duplicate/retention/recovery consequences;
- explicit as-of and nonretroactive exposure limitations, not fictional withdrawal of released copies;
- empirical and operational verification plans proportionate to R-P01–R-P06 before relevant user-facing claims or real operations.

**Left undecided here:** identity provider, client framework, database, schema, service topology, API protocol, auth/session mechanism, encryption/retention implementation, queues, event sourcing, telemetry, statistical libraries, deployment environment and release plan. These are future engineering decisions constrained by the above obligations, not Phase 012 outputs. Do not build a product scaffold or executable tests as evidence of this handoff.
