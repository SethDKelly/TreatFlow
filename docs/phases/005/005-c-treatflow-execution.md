# TreatFlow — 005-C Observation, Stewardship & Revision Synchronization Semantics

## Formal disposition

**005-C COMPLETE WITH EXPLICIT LATER REVIEW — 005-D NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** [Canonical composition rules](../../canonical/composition/observation-stewardship-revision.md) define optional legitimate responsibility recognition (C-01), protected factual correction (C-02) and non-automatically coordinated dispute behaviors (C-03). They preserve each concept's own state/actions and avoid compelling identification, attribution, or revision on every report. The rules are **semantically guarded**: no real-world entitlement/policy has yet been approved. 005-E will stress their chaining/denial behavior and 005-F/G will test coverage/exit.

Inputs: [005-A plan](005-a-treatflow-execution.md), [005-B action surface](005-b-treatflow-execution.md), [Phase 004 exit](../004/004-f-treatflow-exit-review.md), five [concept owners](../../canonical/concepts/candidates.md), and [composition contract](composition-synchronization-contract.md).

## 1. Binding eligibility and missing-value audit

| Apparent relationship | What is legitimately known | Incorrect shortcut and treatment |
|---|---|---|
| ObservationId → evidence report | An account can contain distinguishable qualified EvidenceItems; a Report produces a reference | Account identity ≠ one claim; reject automatic subject registration on Establish |
| EvidenceItem → Stewardship Item | Specific report can be treated as a contributed item if justified in application context | Naming coincidence or shared household cannot assert association; optional C-01 |
| Observer/source → Stewardship Party | Source is origin of an account's claim, not verified private responsible Party | Reject matching household/device/name as an entitlement proof |
| EvidenceItem → Revision Subject/Assertion | A qualified source assertion can be given a revisable subject/initial version if legitimately registered | Reject arbitrary registration of another source's claim or loss of asserted unit/time |
| Standing → amendment right | Protected responsibility may inform whether a person should be considered | Standing is not sufficient authorization; require Revision's own legitimate amender guard |
| Closed Observation → later revision | Close blocks direct new Report/Qualify; Revision can manage separate factual history | Reject reopening or silently overwriting closed Observation as a correction |

No claimed binding assumes shared database keys, API payloads or copied private concept state.

## 2. Tested composition alternatives

- **Mandatory Report→Associate→EstablishSubject:** *rejected*. A distracted volunteer can provide valid group evidence without identifying a Party or creating a revision history. Compulsory association would undermine P-01, and either other participant's guard could fail.
- **Optional Report plus justified Associate:** *accepted conditionally* as C-01; attachment only when real evidence supports Party/Item standing, with separately valid reporting retained after denial.
- **Always establish every Observation report as a Revision Subject:** *rejected*. No independent purpose or legitimate subject-registration basis established universally; C-02 scopes the enrollment to eligible claims rather than hiding automation.
- **Protect factual correction through Revision:** *accepted conditionally* as C-02: preserve predecessor and contested branches, obey Revision authority, and leave Observation's original account unchanged.
- **Delegate correction authority automatically to Stewardship holder:** *rejected*. Association proves neither factual authority nor legitimate standing in all contexts.
- **Collapse all challenges into one cross-concept dispute:** *rejected*. Attribution contest and factual challenge concern separable subject matters and authorities.

## 3. Representative counterexamples

**Two participants, one household:** One Party's `Recognize` may identify only their legitimately associated Item; another Party's report does not thereby become theirs. A contested association never authorizes `Amend`.

**A group corrected from five to four:** Once an eligible qualified source assertion is associated with a Revision Subject, an authorized `Amend` adds the successor and reason. The Observation report remains historically intelligible with its old claim; application interpretations of currentness require later analysis and must not assume deletion of the earlier assertion.

**Closed source account:** No new Observation report or qualifier is added. Authorized historical correction is a separate Revision action, if the registration and amender guards can be met.

**Third-party reliability challenge:** Revision.Challenge may be legitimately permitted without granting amendment. Contesting contributor identity under Stewardship is a separate action and does not alter fact claims.

**Simultaneous incompatible corrections:** neither successor becomes uniquely current by chronology alone; Revision's own contest/currentness behavior prevails.

**No safe entitlement basis:** protected amendment remains unavailable; this is a conceptual denied action, not a deferred automatic operator override.

## 4. Scope and downstream questions

These are conceptual **application participation constraints**, not executable multi-step workflows. C-01 and C-02 each define semantic participation and guard relationships; neither declares an unconditional causal action chain. Phase 005-E must test if additional action exposure or synchronization is actually required for the promised user experience. If necessary input or permission is absent from concept contracts, reopen prior design instead of inventing hidden adapters.

**005-D** handles research qualification, disclosure and historical source changes. **005-E** challenges over-/under-synchronization, simultaneous triggers and authority compatibility. **005-F/G** consolidate coverage and independently audit application action surface.

No authorization, privacy, consent, retention, production collection, or scientific validity is certified; these are later protected obligations.
