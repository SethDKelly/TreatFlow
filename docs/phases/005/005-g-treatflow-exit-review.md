| Application intention | Explanation and guard | Adversarial disposition |

| Optional contribution recognition | C-01 maps specific `EvidenceItem` to generic Item only with independently legitimate Party standing | **PASS, guarded:** no household-derived identity or right |

**Important qualification:** C-01, C-02 and D-01–D-03 are *guarded composition/eligibility semantics*, not universally unconditional event-triggered synchronizations. Where an initiating action is not established, no autonomous downstream action is claimed. This is sufficient for the deliberately limited current application promise **only because** it does not claim automatic creation, notification or release. If a future user experience demands those, reopen 005 for explicit trigger, binding, participants, guard and denial semantics.

## 3. Independence, synchronization and exposure

No current C/D rule rewrites an intrinsic concept action, imports another concept's private state, or makes every concept action public. Application identities `EvidenceItem`, `Item`, `Subject`, `Assertion`, `EvidenceReference`, `Provision` and `Material` remain differentiated and require purpose-justified mappings. Concept valid actions may remain internal or restricted without being missing. No current action requires services, persistence, orchestration, message transports or a transaction protocol to make sense.

**Oversynchronization checks:** Report survives absent/contested contributor standing; a valid Observation need not become Revision.Subject; scientific inquiry can be unsuitable without public release; irrelevant factual change does not suspend everything. **Pass.**

**Undersynchronization checks:** For the promised *bounded* application actions, interpretation/currentness, protected correction and research release have explicit semantic constraints. **Pass with scope limit:** there is no promise of automatic recipient notification, retrieval of past copies, public raw evidence browsing or dynamic global requalification. Adding such promises would be a design gap requiring reentry, not a free implication of D-01–D-03.

**Authority consistency:** actor legitimacy for Associate, Amend, ResolveChallenge, Assess, Decide, MakeAvailable and Provide is a necessary guard, not an actor role magically granted by a related action or an operator title. Where an authority or a protection basis is unavailable, the consequential action cannot occur. **No substantive identity/consent/age/disclosure authorization has been established.**

## 4. Chaining, automation and synergy

The adversarial [005-E chain audit](005-e-treatflow-execution.md) rejects forced Report→Associate→EstablishSubject, standing→Amend, Challenge→Resolve/Amend, Amend→automatic requalification/release, Decide→Provide and changed material→automatic global suspension. No accepted unconditional cyclic reaction exists; no claim of runtime race freedom follows. **Conceptual automation verdict: no unconditional reactive automation established**, and none is fabricated merely to fulfill the phase title.

The [005-F synergy hypothesis](005-f-treatflow-execution.md) is **retained with strong qualification**: combining historically traceable correction, responsible evidence fitness and independently restricted prospective sharing can create an application-level benefit beyond merely placing all three features side by side. This benefit is conceptual and bounded to the guarded combined interpretation; it is not proven user value, anonymity, scientific inference, legality, or an automatic correction service. Phase 006 may decide that a smaller product subset does not need this benefit.

## 5. Purpose/scenario coverage and red-team exclusions

P-01–P-06 and all eight illustrative Phase 001 success situations are traced in [005-F](005-f-treatflow-execution.md). The independent challenge confirms **qualified coverage rather than validated outcomes**: group versus visitor, watched zero versus missing, recalled uncertainty, correction standing, research bias, household nonparticipation and manageable authority scope remain expressible. Particularly:

- A curious public reader has **no blanket entitlement** to source-level evidence or research provision: safe aggregate/public-facing interpretation, if pursued, needs later adopted product scope and disclosure controls.
- A recipient of an older provision is **not promised automatic notification** or a repaired copy after correction.
- A seasonal operator is **not proven capable** of adjudication, privacy review, deletion processing or incident response.
- This exit cannot authorize any actual Halloween data collection, testing on real persons, data transfer or release.

These are explicit product/policy/validation limits, not hidden promises.

## 6. Phase 006 dependency and subset handoff

**Phase 006-A** must define the TreatFlow application family and separate three relationships:

1. **Intrinsic concept dependence (defect):** e.g., if Revision cannot have meaning without Observation in its own specification, reopen Phase 004; do not encode it as a normal dependency.
2. **Composition/synchronization:** e.g., C-02 links Observation and Revision actions when both participate; **does not** itself mean all variants must include both.
3. **Extrinsic inclusion dependence:** a candidate rule `A → B` holds *only when* an application variant including A lacks its intended product role without B. Purpose-justify it; do not infer from the existence of a C/D rule.

**Candidate subset questions, not accepted edges:**
- Is minimal voluntary qualified Observation meaningful without Stewardship, Revision, Research Provision or Controlled Disclosure?
- Could Stewardship be an optional responsible-contributor experience without being compulsory for all reporting?
- If the chosen application promises historical correction, must it also include Observation for this product family, although generic Revision has independent meaning?
- Can Research Provision be a useful inquiry-suitability-only variant without a disclosure/delivery capability?
- If protected research delivery is included, which concept set is required and which existing D rules remain applicable?
- Are scientifically useful but not publicly available evidence, and protected nonresearch disclosures, separate coherent variants?
- Does the community-research mandate justify a particular full subset, rather than accepting every theoretically valid combination?
- Which action-surface and C/D composition rules cease to be relevant in smaller variants, without rewriting intrinsic concepts?

**Do not decide inclusions/edges or draw a full product-family graph here.** [006-A start gate](../006/006-a-start-gate.md) must authorize scope and derive the actual Phase 006 workstreams.

## 7. Unresolved obligations and reopening conditions

**Can carry into Phase 006 concept-family analysis:** actor/recipient experience variants, whether notices belong to an adopted scope, product-family dependence, empirical user benefit, later UI mapping and broader reuse/familiarity.

**Block before collection/operation/release:** lawful participant and guardian/child eligibility, verified protected amendment and adjudicator authority, nonparticipant household/privacy/linkability risk, acceptable disclosure policy, retention/withdrawal/deletion obligations, scientific inference/volunteer-selection limitations, appropriate operator resource and incident model. None is solved by an abstract PASS.

**Must reopen before proceeding beyond conceptual design if encountered:** failed semantic value binding; impossible protected precondition combination; desired automatic effect lacking a trigger/guarded participant rule; new public data or recipient notice promise; implicit shared household identity; conflict between revision/history and authorized material scope. Reopen Phase 003/004 for intrinsic errors or 005 for missing composition, instead of delegating to code.
