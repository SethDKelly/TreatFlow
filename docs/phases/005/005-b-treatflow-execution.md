# TreatFlow — 005-B Application Action Surface, Direct Exposure & Non-Exposure

## Decision

**005-B COMPLETE WITH CARRY-FORWARD — 005-C NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** Every one of the five retained concepts' **29 intrinsic actions** has an explicit provisional application-surface classification in the [single canonical action-surface owner](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md). This phase decides **candidate direct exposure, restricted action boundaries and deliberate non-exposure**. It does **not** define multi-concept synchronizations, legal permission rules, actual actor identities, UI, APIs or implementation.

Inputs: [005-A](005-a-treatflow-execution.md), [Phase 004 exit](../004/004-f-treatflow-exit-review.md), [Phase 005 composition contract](composition-synchronization-contract.md), [current concepts](../../canonical/concepts/candidates.md) and [purpose framing](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md).

## 1. Principal decisions

- **Low-burden observation remains independently possible:** Establish, AssertCoverage, Report, Qualify, Inspect, Close and InspectAfterClose may be offered directly under each intrinsic source/scope/open-state guard. None is made dependent on externally completed Stewardship, Revision, research evaluation or public disclosure.
- **Private contributor standing is individually meaningful:** Recognize, ContestStanding and Relinquish can be offered to a legitimately entitled party. Associate is not publicly offered as a free-standing claim over arbitrary items; legitimate identity-to-item association is an unresolved 005-C composition/authority question. DetermineStanding is restricted to separately legitimate adjudicators.
- **Factual correction is not ownership correction:** InspectHistory and legitimate Challenge can be available by qualified scope, while Amend is protected and its application meaning for Observation requires 005-C. EstablishSubject and MarkSuperseded are not open independent application actions.
- **Sharing is a protected decision, not an unrestricted fetch:** Propose/Assess/Decide/SuspendFuture/InspectDecision have explicit authority constraints; MakeAvailable is not independently public. No abstract action implies sufficient consent or verified anonymization.
- **Research can be evaluated without delivery:** RequestEvidence and qualified InspectProvision are candidate direct actions. PrepareScope and QualifyProvision remain internal. Provide is deliberately not an independent download action and requires separately valid availability later.

## 2. P-01–P-06 purpose and actor coverage

| Purpose | Intended application behavior and actor | Adversarial condition |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 Participation burden | Willing observer may report only known event/time or bounded monitored interval | No mandatory login/association, constant watching or post-submission disclosure |
| P-02 Evidence intelligibility | Observer and legitimately scoped interpreter can inspect coverage, units, uncertainty and factual history | Missing extent ≠ zero; group ≠ visitors; disputed history not silently settled |
| P-03 Scientific honesty | Research inquirer can ask about evidence fitness and receive inconclusive/unsuitable qualification | An inquiry request does not require or grant disclosure |
| P-04 Protection | Eligible protective authority may withhold/suspend prospectively, while contributors/public see only legitimate scopes | No inference that researcher or venue automatically holds identity or release rights |
| P-05 Correction responsibility | Legitimately entitled person can recognize/contest standing or challenge a factual assertion; factual amendment requires separate guard | Same household ≠ same contributor; contest ≠ correction |
| P-06 Sustainable operation | Protected decisions remain bounded and not presumed automatic | No unbounded public adjudication, guaranteed safe release or mandatory real-time operator response |

## 3. Direct versus protected versus composed negative controls

- **Oversynchronization failure:** a standalone event report is prevented unless a stewardship association can immediately be created. **Reject** as an assumed application requirement.
- **Unauthorized amendment failure:** any party recognizing a contribution automatically receives `Amend` rights. **Reject**; later authority design must establish rights explicitly.
- **Raw-release failure:** `Provide` or `MakeAvailable` is independently available to an arbitrary researcher. **Reject**.
- **Scientific overclaim failure:** `InspectProvision` is equivalent to underlying private data access, or guarantees scientific representativeness. **Reject**.
- **Dispute failure:** resolving a factual challenge implicitly rewrites a prior Observation or revokes a third party's copy. **Reject**.
- **Protected-action failure:** declaring `Decide` "operator only" without a legitimate authority criterion is sufficient. **Reject**; not yet authorized in real usage.

## 4. Synchronization obligations and open questions

**005-C must resolve:** how abstract Observation evidence references, Stewardship Item and Revision Subject correspond **when legitimately appropriate**; whether Associate/EstablishSubject occur reactively, conditionally, or remain independent; post-close corrections and the role of independently determined protected amendment entitlement; how to handle failed/contested standing without suppressing legitimate initial observation.

**005-D must resolve:** whether and how protected `MakeAvailable` and `Provide` participate together; what qualifies as evidence material changed by Revision; how a research fitness result can be disclosed without raw evidence; and prospective restriction with previously delivered copies.

**005-E must challenge:** guard compatibility, action chains, an internal action unintentionally made public, accidental user-visible obligations, false auto-correction and circular reactions.

No interim binding or trigger is authorized by this inventory. It intentionally distinguishes a **candidate composed intention** from an accepted application action.
