# TreatFlow — 005-F Synergy, Purpose Coverage, Application-Level Consolidation & Corrective Review

## Decision

**005-F COMPLETE WITH CARRY-FORWARD — 005-G NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** The present [application action surface](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md), [C-01–C-03](../../canonical/composition/observation-stewardship-revision.md) and [D-01–D-03](../../canonical/composition/revision-research-disclosure.md) together provide a **bounded conceptual account** of TreatFlow's six design purposes and eight illustrative Phase 001 situations. This means **conceptual coverage**, not user testing, legal authorization, science validation or implemented behavior.

**Synergy verdict: one qualified compositional benefit**, not universal automation: independently recorded, historically correctable observations can inform appropriately limited research suitability assessments *while* proposed delivery remains subject to independently justified protective disclosure. The additional application-level benefit is **accountable scientific usefulness without treating correction, evidentiary fitness or access authority as interchangeable**. It is a reasoned design hypothesis, not measured user value; unsupported wider synergy and unconditional automation claims are rejected.

The substantive **qualification** is that recipient notification or historical-copy repair after later corrections is **not promised**. If product purpose requires active correction notices, this is a newly proposed behavior requiring re-entry, not silent automatic synchronization. Guarded actors and safe disclosure policy remain unvalidated. [005-G](exit-review-template.md) independently tests whether these carry-forwards are genuinely nonblocking for conceptual Phase 006 entry.

## 3. Compositional synergy examination

**Independent constituent value:** Observation records qualified activity; Revision preserves factual history; Stewardship establishes contestable protected responsibility; Research Provision judges evidence fitness; Controlled Disclosure governs prospective availability.

**Qualified extra benefit:** the composed application can avoid two distinct misleading states at once—treating a superseded observation as current *and* treating research suitability as authority to disclose household-linked material. C-02 + D-01 + D-02 + D-03 make it meaningful to speak of an **authorized, qualified interpretation whose evidentiary standing is traceable**, while preserving earlier released versions and refusing new exposure where authority fails. This cross-purpose coherence is not achievable by merely listing five independent concept features without application-level constraints.

**Limits and negative controls:** no universal automatic updating; no assumption that corrected evidence is available to every analyst; no proof of scientific correctness, anonymization, lawful access, citizen value, or time/cost savings. Merely displaying several concept outputs together is **not** synergy. Attribution tracking by itself is **not** evidence that another user's correction rights have been established.

**Verdict:** **provisional, specifically scoped synergy hypothesis** for responsible traceable scientific use; validate later with real actor/recipient and policy evidence. Phase 005 does not fail if its independent exit reviewers reject the synergy characterization.

## 4. Corrective consolidated application surface

The [canonical application action surface](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md) now links current C-01–C-03 and D-01–D-03 semantics and states the bounded application intentions available **only when their constituent protected guards are independently satisfied**. No global report→registration chain, public correction action, automatic research delivery, or compulsory broadcast has been added.

- **Direct qualified:** Observation reporting/inspection, legitimate factual-history inquiry, and inquiry-suitability requests under existing semantic guards.
- **Protected:** contributor standing decisions, legitimate amendment/challenge, disclosure assessment/authorization, and properly scoped decision inspection.
- **Internal/non-exposed alone:** Associate, EstablishSubject, MarkSuperseded, PrepareScope, QualifyProvision, MakeAvailable and Provide as listed in 005-B. Their presence inside a guarded combined intention does not publicly expose them.
- **Composed guarded:** optional recognition, protected correction, qualified research interpretation, protected research delivery, and future material-scope reassessment; no unconditional chains.

No duplicate universal sync catalog has been created. The C and D natural owners retain the detailed value bindings and guards; the action surface indexes rather than redefines them.

## 5. Unresolved promises and authority reconciliation

**Post-delivery amendment notices:** Explicitly **NOT PROMISED**. An old delivered copy remains what was historically received. Whether authorized recipients may or should be notified of new factual standing needs a separate purpose, actor, information-scope, consent and availability analysis. If promised, reopen the design before relying on existing D-01.

**Public research access:** No entitlement to protected raw observation material; safe public summaries, if eventually chosen, require a separately specified experience and disclosure scope. Do not treat an entitled `InspectProvision` as a universal public analytics capability.

**Insufficient safeguarding basis:** It blocks affirmative release, even when the evidence would scientifically support an inquiry. Do not grant operators blanket rights merely to unblock C-02/D-02/D-03.

**Changed material:** The earlier approval remains evidence for its original scope, not an automatic permission for changed data, audience or version.

**External obligations:** participation and guardian/minor eligibility, source rights, legitimate adjudicators, data retention/deletion, safe release and risk evaluation, external research methodology, operator capacity remain **blocking before real-world collection/release**, but do not establish a missing *conceptual composition* action under the current limited product promises.
