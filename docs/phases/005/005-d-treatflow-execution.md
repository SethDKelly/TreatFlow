# TreatFlow — 005-D Research Qualification, Disclosure & Revision Composition

## Formal disposition

**005-D COMPLETE WITH CARRY-FORWARD — 005-E NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** Three guarded rules now have a [single canonical composition owner](../../canonical/composition/revision-research-disclosure.md):

1. **D-01:** legitimate factual standing informs *prospective* evidence suitability and disclosed qualifications, without silently altering historical provisions.
2. **D-02:** a composed research delivery is available only under both sufficient relevant qualification and a separately valid scoped disclosure authorization. Either finding is independently meaningful.
3. **D-03:** materially changed *future* material/audience scope cannot silently inherit a prior permission; applicable protected reassessment is required, without falsely recalling prior exposures.

These conceptual rules are **not** an automatic `Amend` broadcast, blanket suspension, scientific certification, public download or actual privacy approval. 005-E must verify chaining, guard compatibility and denied states, and 005-F/G independently challenge end-to-end coverage.

## 1. Specificity and value-binding audit

| Distinct semantic identity | Valid correspondence | Rejected shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| `Observation.EvidenceItem` | May be source of qualified asserted content (via 005-C legitimate relationship) | Is automatically the latest corrected truth |
| `Revision.Subject/Assertion` | Factual history/standing for a specifically mapped claim | Automatically controls Research Provision state |
| `ResearchProvision.EvidenceReference/Provision` | Scoped inquiry selection and known historical qualifiers | Means all protected raw reports have been inspected |
| `ControlledDisclosure.Material/Audience/DisclosureDecision` | Scope-specific prospective permission and history | Means every provision is permitted to every researcher |
| `Disclosure.ExposureRecord` | Evidence that past audience/scope exposure occurred | A copy that can be retroactively removed on suspension |

A generic identity is not a permission. Each mapping requires meaningful context, consistent measurement/content scope and legitimately accessible information.

## 2. Tested alternatives and decisions

| Alternative | Counterexample | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Every Revision amendment automatically rebuilds all existing research results | Some revisions are unrelated, inaccessible, disputed or lack authority to change recipient material | **Reject** hidden blanket reaction; adopt guarded D-01 interpretation at qualification time |
| Research fitness directly authorizes delivery | Sample is meaningful but granular data could identify a household or child | **Reject**; protected disclosure remains independently necessary |
| Authorized material release proves research validity | Self-selected reports or unknown monitored gaps still make a Poisson inquiry unfit | **Reject**; Research Provision maintains separate suitability criteria |
| Research `Provide` and Disclosure `MakeAvailable` considered independently public | Both intrinsic actions have guarded eligibility, scoped material and audience | **Reject**; coordinate only under jointly satisfied conditions (D-02) |
| Every factual challenge suspends all disclosures automatically | Disputes may be irrelevant or unresolved and a challenger may have no release authority | **Reject** mandatory blanket suspension; guarded scope reassessment (D-03) |
| Future suspension removes distributed copies | Historical recipient distribution cannot be undone by conceptual action | **Reject** retroactive recall |
| Changed material is distributed under an old unchanged decision | Original approval need not cover revised content or audience | **Reject**; prospective changed scope needs appropriate renewed consideration |

## 3. Scenario evidence

**Corrected group size:** A source assertion about five people becomes a legitimate four-person successor. Original Observation and Revision history remain intact. A newly prepared research qualification should use the known appropriate standing, with uncertainty and dispute retained. Historical delivered provisions do not change merely because a revision occurred.

**Scientific value but unsafe release:** A carefully scoped inquiry may be scientifically meaningful even if dense timestamps and location combinations would expose nonparticipant households. Qualification cannot force `Decide` or `MakeAvailable`. No raw provision follows.

**Approved release, unusable inference:** A previously approved disclosure may exist for limited material, yet monitored time is too incomplete for a proposed Poisson model. `QualifyProvision` may return unsuitable/inconclusive. Permission does not prove suitability.

**Changed content after release:** A prior audience receives an approved historical form. A material change requires reconsideration before a **future newly scoped** exposure. The old exposure remains factually recorded; no delete or recall promise arises.

**Unknown evidence standing:** No automatic current-truth assessment is inferred when an authorized conceptual source of standing or relevant evidence is unavailable. The result must reflect unknown or unqualified limitations.

## 4. Interaction integrity and authority

D-01–D-03 preserve each concept's intrinsic purpose and guards. They do not grant a research actor release authority, a disclosure authority scientific expertise, or an Observation reporter amendment rights. Any disclosure authorization depends on substantive protection conditions not established by Phase 005; **these diagrams and rules cannot be used as operational permission**.

D-02 is explicitly a guarded **application-level multi-concept availability constraint**. D-01 and D-03 are narrower interpretation/prospective decision obligations; neither states an unconditional causal trigger that would bypass missing values or protected actors. This distinction is essential for 005-E's later composition audit.
