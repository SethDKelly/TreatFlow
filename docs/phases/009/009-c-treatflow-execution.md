## 3. D-01, D-02 and D-03 chain tests

**D-01 — legitimate as-of research qualification.** V history can contain an original assertion, one successor and an unresolved fork. R.PrepareScope/QualifyProvision considers only legitimately inspectable standing, preserves known coverage/unit/selection limitations and qualifies a *new* provision accordingly. A previously delivered/inspected provision remains an as-of artifact; no automatic refresh or third-party notification is invented. **R purpose preserved** because unfavorable/unknown fitness is a valid result and source history remains unchanged.

**D-02 — independent dual guard.** The conceptual research-delivery intention needs an R-appropriate qualified scope *and* a separately defensible D decision covering precisely the Material/Audience. If either side fails, neither R.Provide nor D.MakeAvailable is available through the composed intention. This does **not** suppress meaningful independent R assessment or prevent a distinct eligible D decision for nonresearch content in another context. **Both purposes preserved**; treating the guard as automatic delivery would violate both.

**D-03 — prospective scope changes and recipients.** An amended assertion, altered R qualification or proposed new Audience can affect a contemplated future release only when material correspondence is justified. The previous decision cannot authorize changed material/audience; the changed scope remains unavailable pending qualified D assessment/decision. Existing ExposureRecord and prior recipients' copies stay historical. D.SuspendFuture acts only prospectively, within legitimate protected authority; no automatic notice, recall or global shutdown arises. **D purpose preserved** without fabricating retroactive guarantees.

| **D-03** | D can decide and suspend under intrinsic rules | Materially changed future release could reuse stale approval or imply historical copy deletion; P-04/P-06 degraded |

**Historical timeline:** report group encounter → legitimate V successor changes count → R inquiry initially qualified under known-as-of standing → D assesses a matching material/audience scope → possible protected exposure with caveats → new contest or materially changed requested audience → future R/D status requires new legitimately supported judgment → old ExposureRecord and any delivered copy remain historical. The stages are **possible guarded events**, not a guaranteed automatic workflow.

## 5. Integrity-vs-policy and reentry assessment

**Confirmed purpose-defeating compositional defect:** none demonstrated **within the existing guards**. No missing intrinsic Research Provision or Controlled Disclosure action or contradictory synchronization is presently established.
