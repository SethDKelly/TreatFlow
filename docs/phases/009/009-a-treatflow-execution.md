## 3. Coverage model: pairwise, clusters and chronology

**Directional pairwise coverage:** deliberately include O⇄S, O⇄V, O⇄R, O⇄D, S⇄V, S⇄D, V⇄R, V⇄D, R⇄D, and S⇄R where authority/provenance materially reaches evidence qualification. A listed pair indicates a *question to test*, not a previously established sync or direct state dependency; record N/A with explicit rationale where no credible channel exists.

**Mandatory cluster coverage:**
- **O–S–V (C-01–C-03):** report without standing; challenge standing; correction after closure; simultaneous disagreements.
- **O–V–R–D (D-01–D-03):** historical correction → prospective qualification → dual-guard release → changed audience → prior recipient retains as-of data.
- **O–S–V–R–D whole family:** one observer, another affected household member, rival contributor claim, researcher and decision-maker with different entitlements; constrained/no-delivery paths.
- **Temporal replay:** pre-account, watched quiet/unwatched interval, report, close, contested revision, stale inquiry, denied/approved prospective decision, actual exposure, later scope change and suspension. Distinguish a possible sequence from a promised automatic chain.

Audit each concept as both **affected subject** and potential **interfering neighbor**. Local correctness alone does not satisfy full-family integrity.

## 4. Test method and evidence classes

For each test record (a) concept purpose and legitimate actor, (b) native valid starting state and target, (c) applicable guarded application action/synchronization, (d) composed effect or exclusion, (e) competing interpretation or violation attempt, (f) expected invariant/authority/temporal result, (g) actual *documented* result, (h) disposition, (i) upstream correction owner, and (j) affected other tests. Distinguish **proven contradiction**, **locally unsupported new requirement**, **unresolved policy/empirical risk**, **nonapplicable interaction**, and **supported preservation**. Negative tests and counterfactual removal of a synchronization are required to identify directionality.

Evidence: **canonical semantic proof/trace**, **documented counterexample**, **uncertainty/carry-forward**, **specification reentry/repair with exact-head verification** if needed. A checklist assertion, popularity, runtime mock or invented actor entitlement is not evidence. No code/runtime testing is authorized.

## 5. Phase 009 project-specific subphases

**009-B — Observer Independence, Standing, Revision & Protected Authority Integrity.** Analyze O–S–V and C-01–C-03, low-burden path, Party/Observer separation, legitimation and closure/post-close history. Gate: document purpose-survival and any directional interference with owning repair.

**009-C — Research Fitness, Disclosure Permission & Recipient Integrity.** Analyze O/R/D, D-01–D-03, negative and as-of research conditions, dual-guard delivery, future-scope change and historical copies. Gate: protection and scientific qualification never substitute for each other.

**009-D — Cross-Concept Chains, Temporal State, Disputes & Authority Interference.** Replay long O–S–V–R–D chains and counterfactual effects. Gate: all chained downstream outcomes and historical states remain coherent; unresolved real policy is classified, not invented.

**009-E — Whole-Family Coverage, Scope/Mapping/Familiarity & Purpose Preservation.** Review all material directed pair and cluster combinations, all 29 application-action exposure categories, Phase 006 single-family restrictions, Phase 007 integrated semantic visibility and 008-C/E false-familiarity/reuse carry-forwards. Gate: complete five-concept purpose matrix, justified N/A cells and no untested critical interference.

**009-F — Misfit Adjudication, Upstream Repairs, Propagation & Regression.** Determine confirmed versus hypothetical gaps; if confirmed, reopen specific Phase 001/003/004/005/006/007/008 canonical owners, repair, rerun upstream and downstream scenarios; otherwise document evidence-qualified no-change. Gate: zero known purpose-defeating uncorrected interference.

**009-G — Independent Integrity Exit, Documentation Audit & Phase 010 Handoff.** Challenge every earlier PASS, review knowledge authority/OKF, current-status/index consistency and Phase 010 adverse-scenario agenda. Gate: no unresolved confirmed integrity breach, explicit separate policy/empirical limitations, formal Phase 010 entry decision.

**Dependency order:** B/C may supply separate local cluster evidence → D combined temporal/authority chains → E whole-family completeness → F remediate/verify → G independent exit. Each subphase gets a traceable historical execution record; any accepted new current rule belongs only in its natural canonical owner.

## 6. Upstream reentry and phase boundaries

An undefined intrinsic action/state belongs to **003**, unsound independent factoring to **004**, an invalid or missing synchronization/application action to **005**, wrong family/inclusion rule to **006**, misleading mapping/availability to **007**, and an adopted familiar concept/genericity error to **008**; revisit **001** if the purported purpose itself no longer applies. Phase **009** diagnoses and validates composed purpose failure and coordinates propagation, not shadow specifications. Phase **010** handles wider exceptional/misuse/domain adversarial validation *after* known structural integrity issues are corrected.

Unresolved before operation: legitimate reporter/guardian authority and protected adjudicators, household and visitor reidentification, material/audience release safety, consent/withdrawal/retention, inferential science/selection and staffing/incident handling. The audit can label their consequences without manufacturing real policy. **No actual data collection, release, interface architecture, API or code is authorized.**
