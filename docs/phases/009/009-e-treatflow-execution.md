## 4. Scope, variants and dependence integrity

- Exactly **one adopted integrated {O,S,V,R,D} native design family**. Independent O reporting, absent S/V involvement, R unsuitable/no delivery and D denial/suspension are legitimate **experience states**, not independently offered editions.
- Exactly four contextual native inclusion edges **S→O, V→O, R→O, D→O** (application roles). Neither O→S nor unconditional R↔D or universal concept dependence follows.

- Reduced participation must not falsify P-01, and the presence of a family concept cannot trigger all 29 actions for every encounter.

## 5. Mapping, familiarity and actor-relative knowledge

The four Phase 007 owners retain (i) monitoring coverage/precision/units, (ii) protected Party responsibility versus factual Revision/branches, (iii) science fitness versus prospective D decision/actual exposure, and (iv) combined action availability, uncertainty, status, and noncolor-only distinctions.

**Hard combined-view test:** a researcher entitled only to qualified evidence summaries asks whether records are “current” while a private V challenge may exist. The representation must *not* disclose hidden challenge or assert complete present truth. An entitled scoped result may say **standing not fully established**, **qualification limited to accessible evidence**, or **unavailable for the requested inference**, without falsely revealing private source details. A D decision does not make that R assessment more certain. This is compatible with R's meaningful unsuitable/unavailable outcome **provided the interface never claims complete unopposed currentness**. Empirical clarity remains untested.

Phase 008 false-familiarity checks block user-facing “owner = editor”, “edit = overwritten history”, “qualified = scientifically proven”, “approved = published”, “revoked = prior copies erased” and “anonymous = safe”. The five reuse candidate dispositions are **limited transfer knowledge**, not imported alternative concept implementations or mandatory standards.
