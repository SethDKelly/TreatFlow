## 3. Action, state, authority, temporal and automation audit

- **Actions and effects:** 29 intrinsic actions remain classified in the current [application action surface](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md). Direct/qualified, protected, internal and composed-participant classifications are not universal access rights. O.Report survives S refusal; V.Amend is not a destructive O action; R.Provide and D.MakeAvailable are independently guarded participants.
- **State and invariant compatibility:** Coverage unknown is not zero; distinct S standing and V truth disputes do not settle each other; competing V successors have no automatic winner; R and D states remain independent.
- **Authority:** O source, S Party, V authorized amender, R entitled inspector, D assessor/decider and recipient are not equated. A protected action without established legitimacy remains unavailable rather than delegated by convenience.
- **Temporal integrity:** O.Close vs later V successor, old R qualification vs new known factual history, future changed D scope, D.SuspendFuture vs past ExposureRecord remain separate.
- **Chained composition:** C-01–C-03 and D-01–D-03 do not impose automatic S→V→R→D triggering, global recall or recipient notification. Removing critical guards reveals why combined behavior requires them.
- **Mapping and familiarity:** The four Phase 007 owners preserve observed/unknown, contributor/owner, amendment/challenge, qualified/approved/published, and suspended/erased distinctions. Phase 008 did not adopt misleading substitute concepts or a parallel authoritative catalog.
- **Scope:** Four native context-qualified S/V/R/D→O edges are not intrinsic universal dependencies; R without delivery and D decline are valid design outcomes.

**Independent negative recheck:** O reports without S/V; a household contributor challenges another's standing; closed O gains a legitimate V successor with a competing branch; a researcher cannot inspect private V history and must report epistemic limits; an R-qualified provision is blocked by D; D approval does not repair missing measurement exposure; modified future audience is denied under stale approval; suspended access does not erase previous recipient copies. The canonical guards prohibit the corresponding purpose violations; this does **not** amount to runtime or empirical verification.

## 6. Phase 010 entry and priority adversarial agenda

Upon merge, **010-A — Validation Scope, Misfit Hypotheses, Risk Coverage & Subphase Planning** becomes next eligible. Its task is to prioritize representative success and context-misfit scenarios; it does **not** inherit an approved testing implementation plan. Proposed high-value cases:

1. Successful low-effort report with observed quiet, unobserved gaps, one group containing multiple people, and imprecise recollection; ensure honest research interpretation later.
2. Different contributors in one household, minor/guardian uncertainty, contested contribution and contested factual assertion; ensure no authority is conjured from household association.
3. Legitimate post-close correction after earlier qualified inquiry/release; examine inability to reach past recipients and understandable stale knowledge.
4. Researcher requests scientific conclusion from sparse voluntary evidence; assess epistemic failure modes and selection/monitoring bias without promising Poisson proof.
5. Protected approval requested with insufficient linkability/risk basis for residents, visitors or observers; test withheld/nonavailability paths and source minimization.
6. Changed audience/material, repeated releases, derived aggregates and linkability over time; check scope, third-party impacts and prospective restrictions.
7. Contradictory/late/recalled reports, missing actor entitlement and denied actions; test mistake, challenge, recovery, ambiguity and clear status.
8. Small-volunteer operator's inability to resolve a standing/disclosure case promptly; test conceptual unresolved/pending outcomes rather than assuming automated adjudication.

Any Phase 010 discovery of intrinsic/scope/composition/mapping **structural** interference must reenter its 003–009 natural owner and repeat the affected purpose-preservation audit. Phase 010 must also test *representative success*, not merely negative scenarios; its exit feeds methodology closure in Phase 011.
