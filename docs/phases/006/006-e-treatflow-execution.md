## 1. Concept-level inclusion versus occurrence-level participation

The five concepts are included in the **chosen design family**; that does **not** force all five to participate in one experience. Variant-like states listed below are **restricted pathways within the single adopted family**, not newly adopted products or independently implemented subsets. Presence of S, V, R and D does not reverse the context-qualified arrows pointing to O, and their inclusion does not grant a reporter any of their protected actions.

## 3. Dependency edge audit against actions

- **S → O native role** identifies the *kind of target* whose private contribution standing is managed; it does **not** make O.Report depend on S.Associate.
- **V → O native role** identifies the factual assertion subject; it does **not** enroll every O item in Revision nor change O.Close.
- **R → O native role** identifies which evidence R evaluates; it does **not** grant access to O's private state, automatically run R, or require a successful D action.
- **D → O native role** identifies protected material being considered; it does **not** authorize disclosure, require research suitability, or make all observations D decisions.
- **No R ↔ D inclusion edge:** D-02 is an *action-level guarded joint participation* for delivery. Distinguish this from R-only suitability/no-delivery and D-only protective withholding inside the full family.

Every arrow is a contextual **inclusion statement**, not a runtime invocation or grant. The same symbols remain independent abstract concept domains; mapping of an O item into S, V, R or D requires a justified application-level reference and the relevant guard.

## 5. Phase 007 explanation/mapping handoff

Phase 007 should explain the chosen **one integrated family** without drawing independent products from the reduced-participation states. Explain Observation first as native evidence context; then protected responsibility and factual history separately, research fitness independently of release, and disclosure with prospective-only restrictions. A reader should not infer role authority from concept inclusion or assume a simple arrow means execution order. Maps/experiences must reflect intentionally non-exposed participant actions and denied/unknown outcomes. This is **explanation order**, not module or development sequence.

Retain these explicit limits: qualified direct report is possible without S/V involvement, R may say unsuitable without D delivery, D may deny independently of R fitness, past copies do not disappear, and no universal public research data view exists.
