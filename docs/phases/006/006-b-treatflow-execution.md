## 3. Decisive counterexamples and scope distinctions

**Distracted reporting:** Removing S, V, R and D does not defeat O's conceptual reporting purpose. P-01 forbids using S or V as mandatory preconditions. But an observation-only variant may still fail the *whole* TreatFlow mandate's P-03/P-04 ambitions; 006-D decides scope rather than inserting false O dependencies.

**Two contributors, same home:** C-01 preserves independently legitimate Party/Item standing; S's native role requires native contributed items, not proof of real-world identity or control by address. Without a valid Party, O reporting remains intact.

**Late correction without public person identity:** V's native role is correcting eligible O assertions, not automatically importing source identity. Its application dependence on O does not permit `Amend` without independent entitlement or require all O entries to become Subjects.

**Evidence inquiry, no delivery:** R can qualify absence/unknown evidence and refuse inference without D. Whether it can be meaningfully included without O in this *specific* family is unresolved pending the variant purpose.

**Nonresearch protected information:** D can make legitimate prospective restrictions without R. Its target may be material other than Observation in another context, so no global D→O edge follows.

**Past release corrected:** C-02 and D-01/D-03 define guarded coordination when relevant participants are included; their existence alone does not imply universal inclusion, retroactive copy repair, or automatic notifications.

## 4. Accepted, rejected and unresolved authority boundaries

- **Accepted now:** only S→O and V→O for specifically qualified native-evidence application roles. The same generic concepts may appear alone in another application family. Both remain provisional until 006-C falsification.
- **Rejected now:** O→S, O→V, S→V, V→S, R→D and D→R as global requirements. No C/D synchronization is promoted automatically to a dependency.
- **Unresolved:** R→O and D→O in named contexts; protected research delivery's *conditional joint inclusion* and any novel variant from 006-C.
- **No intrinsic concept defect established.** If a downstream test discovers such a defect, reopen Phase 004; do not add a dependency to conceal it.
