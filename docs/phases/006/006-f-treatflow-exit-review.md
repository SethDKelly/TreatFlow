## 2. Correct relationship types and edge rationale

The adopted *native evidence application family* uses O = Observation, S = Contribution Stewardship, V = Revision, R = Research Provision and D = Controlled Disclosure.

**Context-qualified extrinsic inclusion edges:** **S → O, V → O, R → O and D → O**, each because its *chosen role* concerns TreatFlow-native observation contributions, factual assertions, inquiry evidence or protected observation-derived material. Remove O and those **particular native roles** lack their target; the generic independently specified concepts still make sense in other families.

**Explicit nonedges:** O does **not** depend on S/V/R/D; no S↔V, R↔D, compulsory S/V enrollment, protected authorization via inclusion or code-level relation is entailed. **D-02's coordinated research delivery** requires eligible R and D actions *when chosen*, but does not imply unconditional R→D or D→R concept inclusion. No accepted intrinsic dependence or internal cross-concept state was established. If mapping shows one, reopen Phase 003/004.

## 3. Subset validity, closure and cycles

Within the adopted **native-role** context, X is edge-valid iff every included S, V, R or D has O included. Thus closure({S})={S,O}, closure({V})={V,O}, closure({R})={R,O}, closure({D})={D,O}, closure({S,V,R,D})={O,S,V,R,D} and closure({O})={O}. This is the final contextual dependency model, **not** a universal assertion about generic S/V/R/D concepts.

Examples:
- **Edge-valid but not separate adopted products:** {O}, {O,S}, {O,V}, {O,R}, {O,D}, {O,R,D}, {O,S,V}.
- **Invalid within specified native roles:** {S}, {V}, {R} or {D} without O when promising to operate on *TreatFlow-native observations*.
- **Coherent elsewhere, out of scope:** generic external-evidence suitability {R} or protected non-observation material {D}; generic standalone S/V over unrelated targets.
- **Adopted conceptual scope:** {O,S,V,R,D}, subject to each action's guard and the possibility of legitimate denied/unavailable/no-delivery outcomes.

No accepted multi-hop closure requirement beyond O and no directed cycle. The earlier 006-C classification of generic {R}/{D} as potentially meaningful is not contradicted by their **different native-role invalidity**. **Dependency-valid does not mean user-valued, safe, operational or adopted**.

## 4. Product mandate, counterexamples and scope sufficiency

The [scope owner](../../canonical/project/concept-family-scope.md) adopts one integrated design to cover P-01–P-06 together: low-friction observation, qualified evidence, private accountability, responsible exploratory interpretation, nonparticipant protection and sustainable stewardship obligations. No separate editions, marketplace, external evidence service, public source-level exploration or unguarded raw-data delivery are adopted.

Under that one design, direct observation may occur without S/V/R/D actions. A research inquiry can be unsuitable without permission or delivery. D can decline future availability independently, and a legitimate correction must preserve recorded history without forcibly repairing historical recipient copies. This selection is a **design intent**, not a mandate to collect/release real data or evidence of user/operational fit.

**Scope tension review:** all five concepts may increase complexity for a small seasonal operator, but their independent behaviors reflect the currently adopted sponsor purposes and protective obligations. This remains **an unvalidated feasibility burden**, not evidence that dropping a concept is safe. Reassess in later empirical/policy work if the operating model cannot sustain the chosen scope.

## 5. Variant-sensitive composition and upstream integrity

[006-E](006-e-treatflow-execution.md) checks unassociated reporting, private recognition, contested standing, corrections in open/closed Observation, disputed Revision branches, R qualification without D delivery, D denial without research certainty, guarded protected R+D delivery, and changed prospective material. Concept inclusion never triggers one of these actions or grants standing. None of the current *bounded promised behaviors* requires an unowned Phase 005 action.

**Important negative result:** Phase 006 has not justified automatic requalification, historical recipient notification, global suspension, public download, new research data source ingestion or continuous researcher access. A mapped experience promising any of these must reopen 005 and possibly 001/003/004; it must not be invented in Phase 007 views.

## 7. Phase 007 mapping handoff

**007-A — Mapping Scope, Representation Semantics, Experience Risk & Subphase Planning** should derive its own workstreams after this PR merges. It must map **one integrated family** while distinguishing:
- Observer activity, coverage, group-vs-visitor measurement and unknown intervals; a simple direct report must not imply login or automated research/release.
- Private stewardship standing versus factual Revision authority, post-close history and unresolved disagreement.
- Inquiry suitability/limitations versus whether evidence is permitted to be seen or provided.
- Denied or unqualified outcomes and changed future disclosure scope versus historical availability.
- Scope-specific action discoverability/availability and protected-actor guards; no public raw-observation entitlement.

**Explanation-order consideration (not interface order):** O provides native evidence context; S/V explain responsibility and correction without mandatory coupling; R and D have distinct roles, with D-02 release participation only when appropriate. Do not mechanically convert dependency arrows to screens, navigation, source modules or execution order.
