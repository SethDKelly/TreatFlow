# TreatFlow — 006-C Coherent Subsets, Minimal Useful Contexts, Closure & Cycle Challenge

## Decision

**006-C COMPLETE WITH CARRY-FORWARD — 006-D NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** The two [006-B provisional contextual dependencies](../../canonical/dependencies/concept-dependence.md), **Contribution Stewardship → Observation** and **Revision → Observation**, survive removal tests **only** for the *TreatFlow-native observation contribution/correction roles*. Neither is universal across alternative concept applications. No further unconditional inclusion edge, transitive chain or co-inclusion cycle is justified. This review does **not** adopt any variant, prove a lawful real product, or make a coherent subset satisfy all six TreatFlow purposes.

Inputs: [006-A](006-a-treatflow-execution.md), [006-B](006-b-treatflow-execution.md), [Phase 006 contract](dependence-subset-contract.md), [Phase 005 exit](../005/005-g-treatflow-exit-review.md), [canonical purpose scenarios](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md).

## 1. Family-bound validity rule

Use O = Observation, S = Contribution Stewardship, V = Revision, R = Research Provision, D = Controlled Disclosure. Within the **native-contribution/correction role context**, a subset X is edge-valid under presently supported edges iff **S ∈ X implies O ∈ X** and **V ∈ X implies O ∈ X**. This says nothing about the independent generic meanings of S/V outside the native family, the scientific usefulness of X, or the validity of real consent/disclosure. No R/D edges are yet accepted. The rule is deliberately conditional on application role; a single global validity verdict would be misleading for unfamiliar external-evidence contexts.

## 3. Direct falsification of 006-B edges

**S → O:** Remove O while claiming the application lets users recognize responsibility for *TreatFlow-reported observations*. The intended target disappears, so the native-purpose edge stands. Substituting an unrelated Item can rescue generic Stewardship but changes the family role; it does not defeat the qualified edge. Conversely, O without S still reports a group legitimately: **O → S rejected**.

**V → O:** Remove O while promising factual correction of *TreatFlow-reported arrival claims*. No native assertion remains to correct, so the qualified edge stands. Generic factual histories are possible elsewhere, and an O without V can still make qualified original observations: **O → V rejected**.

**R → O challenge:** An inquiry may evaluate evidence from another legitimate source or conclude that evidence is unavailable. Therefore an unconditional R → O edge is **not justified**; whether the adopted TreatFlow-native research variant requires O remains for 006-D. A perpetual no-evidence assessment does not by itself demonstrate an attractive/useful product.

**D → O challenge:** A protected material decision does not intrinsically demand observations. But a TreatFlow-native observation release variant would have an observational target. Resolve contextual scope before declaring D → O; **006-D pending**, not an accepted edge.

**R ↔ D challenge:** Suitability without delivery and protected nonresearch disclosure are independent counterexamples; neither direction is globally required. If a **delivery action** is included, D-02 requires both *as action participants*, which is not itself a universal inclusion dependency.

## 4. Closure, optionality and cycle reasoning

Under the two qualified native-role edges, **closure({S}) = {S,O}** and **closure({V}) = {V,O}**, whereas **closure({S,V}) = {S,V,O}**. Closure({O}) remains {O}. No accepted edge leaves O, R or D, so there is **no multi-hop transitive extension** beyond these direct edges and **no directed cycle**.

Tested speculative cycles and rejected them:
- **O ↔ S:** requiring standing before reporting contradicts low-burden observation.
- **O ↔ V:** requiring revision history for every original report is unjustified.
- **R ↔ D:** no-delivery fitness and nonresearch withholding are distinct independent outcomes.
- **S ↔ V:** a contest about responsibility is not factual correction and vice versa.

Cycles may be appropriate in other application families; **none is justified by current TreatFlow purpose evidence**. O, R and D are not thereby deemed universally optional or universally required; optionality is relative to a *specific coherent variant* and its role, with scope still undecided.

## 5. Boundary and safety checks

- No subset compels registration before Observation.Report or automatically registers every report as Revision.Subject.
- S/V without O is **role-invalid in the native-evidence context**, not intrinsically invalid generic concept design.
- `Provide` cannot become public merely because R and D are co-included; each protected guard remains independent.
- Unknown monitored periods cannot become observed zero in a reduced subset; R cannot certify representative sampling.
- Removing D from a no-delivery R inquiry is conceptually possible; removing D from **composed protected delivery** removes that specific promised action rather than magically waiving permission.
- Earlier exposures persist after correction or future suspension.
- Real participant/guardian consent, minors, households, research method, release authority, retention and operator viability are **not validated by subset tests**.

## 6. Phase 006-D and later handoff

**006-D must decide**, rather than infer from graph validity, which observer/reporting, private standing/correction, suitability-only, protected-delivery and broader integrated variants are truly **adopted in scope**. It must settle whether R's role is strictly native observation evidence (potential contextual R→O), whether D targets only observation material (potential contextual D→O), and whether delivery is promised at all. Select deliberately and distinguish a full-purpose project variant from coherent-but-out-of-scope subsets.

**006-E:** once adopted scope is clear, check every included/excluded concept against 005's action surface and C/D rules; return upstream if a relevant action promise is missing or a concept invariant is violated. **006-F:** independently exit with canonical knowledge and Phase 007 handoff. None is performed here.
