# TreatFlow — 006-D Product-Family Variants, Adopted Scope & Intentional Exclusions

## Decision

**006-D COMPLETE WITH CARRY-FORWARD — 006-E NEXT ELIGIBLE AFTER MERGE.** Adopt **one integrated five-concept native-observation/research family as conceptual design scope**, not five mandatory actions on every report and not a commitment to real-world publication. Retain independently useful *restricted paths* within that family—observation without identity/revision, research assessment without delivery and protective denial without scientific fitness certification. Explicitly **do not adopt** standalone product editions, externally sourced evidence services, unrelated nonresearch sharing or general public raw-data access.

The [canonical scope owner](../../canonical/project/concept-family-scope.md) carries current adopted/unadopted meanings. The [existing dependency owner](../../canonical/dependencies/concept-dependence.md) now includes context-qualified **R → O** for native evidence qualification and **D → O** for native observational material sharing, beside the previously established S → O and V → O. None of these are universal intrinsic concept relationships, and neither R → D nor D → R is warranted as a global inclusion edge.

**Authority:** [006-A planning](006-a-treatflow-execution.md), [006-B role audit](006-b-treatflow-execution.md), [006-C subset/cycle tests](006-c-treatflow-execution.md), [Phase 005 exit](../005/005-g-treatflow-exit-review.md), [project intake](../../canonical/project/intake.md), [P-01–P-06](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md), [Phase 006 dependency contract](dependence-subset-contract.md).

## 1. Scope selection rationale and tradeoffs

The intake's combined purpose asks for **community-contributed qualified Halloween arrival evidence usable for exploratory research under credible protections**, with accountable correction and manageable effort. A full integrated *design* appropriately retains O/S/V/R/D as distinct meaning even when data is withheld. Choosing **only O** would preserve a meaningful recording subset but omit the research/protection and correction goals as *designed capability*. Choosing only O+R would preserve a no-delivery inquiry but leave the project without a controlled path should protected sharing eventually be justified. Choosing O+R+D while excluding S/V would reduce responsibility/correction semantics beyond the sponsor's stated intent; it can be coherent but is not adopted as this project's complete design.

This is **scope rationale, not empirical user validation**. Including D documents meaningful refusals/restrictions before any approval for actual sharing. The system cannot claim safe public access merely from inclusion. Reporters need not be identified to provide each valid claim; standing and amendments remain independently guarded and optional per assertion.

## 2. Selected and deliberately excluded variants

| Candidate | Test and disposition |
|---|---|
| O-only reporting | **Coherent**; retain independent reporting *inside* integrated family, not a separate product commitment |
| O+S / O+V / O+S+V | **Coherent**; no separate edition or variant adopted |
| O+R qualification without D | **Coherent**; *adopt no-delivery qualification pathway* within integrated family without granting protected evidence |
| O+R+D | **Coherent guarded** delivery-oriented subset, but no separately offered edition |
| Full O+S+V+R+D | **Adopt as conceptual family scope** with denial/unknown outcomes valid; release only a conditional intention |
| External-only R, unrelated-material-only D, unrelated S/V | Generic coherent alternatives, **out of adopted TreatFlow scope** |
| Public raw data, third-party visitor tracking, mandatory stable identity, automatic copy recall | **Not adopted**; contradicts present evidence, protective boundary or product purpose |

None is asserted to be an implemented application, market tier, rollout, revenue plan or permission to use any person's information.

## 3. Newly qualified contextual edges

| Qualified edge | Why justified *in the selected family* | Counterexample preserving generic independence |
|---|---|---|
| **R → O** | Chosen Research Provision role characterizes the fitness of **TreatFlow-native observational evidence**, not imported external collections | Generic Research Provision may evaluate other qualified references or conclude unavailable; the native role disappears without O |
| **D → O** | Chosen Controlled Disclosure role governs prospective availability **of TreatFlow observation-derived material** | A generic D instance can govern other material elsewhere; even in adopted family D can decline disclosure without R |

**S → O** and **V → O** remain as previously scoped to native contribution standing and correction. Thus the current contextual inclusion rule for the chosen native family is: inclusion of **S, V, R or D for its adopted native role requires O**, but including O does not automatically require the other four. A full five-concept design is deliberately adopted to cover purposes rather than mandated by inclusion closure alone.

**Reject:** R → D and D → R unqualified. R can return unsuitable/no-delivery; D can decline/limit without research provision. D-02 only coordinates conditional protected research delivery and does not prove a universal role dependence.

## 4. Case-level product meaning and contradiction checks

**Distracted observer:** direct qualified report survives unregistered party or unavailable research assessment. Adopted scope cannot erase this independence.

**Corrected group-size claim:** eligible Revision can retain historical factual standing, without automatically triggering R or D actions and without rewriting original Observation.

**Inconclusive research, release withheld:** R can legitimately produce qualified/unsuitable result even when D denies availability. Neither role becomes valueless simply because the other has not taken a positive action.

**Protected evidence availability:** D-02 needs correctly scoped R and D eligibility *if and only if* protected research delivery is attempted; this is not a standing promise that a download will be provided.

**Nonparticipating household or child:** the product's inclusion of Controlled Disclosure does not certify that protected release is lawful or safe; absent defensible criteria, no actual release.

**Public reader:** safe aggregate public interpretation is not a broadly exposed raw-record capability. No new public viewer feature invented at 006-D.

## 5. 006-E revalidation and limits

006-E must independently test the five-concept chosen scope and each *experience state* (unassociated reporting, disputed correction, no-delivery research, denial/withholding and optional protected release) against [Phase 005 action surface](../../canonical/composition/application-action-surface.md) and the canonical C/D rules. It must verify that adding R → O and D → O does not inadvertently require every O to be qualified/released or silently alter any concept's intrinsic specification. Missing composed behavior reopens 005 rather than being hidden in an inclusion relation.

006-F must assess whether the integrated adopted scope is proportionate to small-operator burden and whether real operational blockers and unsupported commercial/public promises are clearly stated. **Phase 007** owns later mapping/explanation, not implementation.
