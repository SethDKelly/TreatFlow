## 1. Context and family question

**Family under examination:** TreatFlow as a voluntary, bounded Halloween activity-observation and responsible exploratory-research concept system. Test whether independently meaningful capabilities can be included selectively for **coherent conceptual application variants** while maintaining the purpose tensions around participation burden, reliable evidence, nonparticipant privacy, protected correction, and operator viability.

The phase will distinguish:
- **Potential subsets:** observation-only reporting, observation with protected responsibility/correction, inquiry-suitability without evidence delivery, protected research delivery, and broader integrated responsible research.
- **Contextual variants:** an observer-oriented experience, a guarded research-evaluation context, a protected contributor-management context, and a bounded public interpretation context.
- **Scope:** these are **hypotheses** for dependency analysis, not offered products, feature tiers, separate sites, customer editions or deployment choices.

The chosen TreatFlow mandate includes honest exploratory analysis **and** minimizing harm. Thus a formally dependence-valid observation-only subset need not satisfy the *full product's* research/protection aims. Conversely, a generic concept may be meaningful alone despite having a restricted role in this particular product family. These distinctions are the main subject of Phase 006.

## 2. Relationship meanings — stop rules

| Relationship | Meaning | Correct treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic dependence defect** | One concept requires another's private state or identity to be defined or perform its purpose | Reopen 003/004. Never legitimize by adding a Phase 006 arrow |
| **Composition** | Two independently defined concept actions interact when both are present, as in conditional C-02/D-02 | Phase 005 owns the semantic action relationship; not sufficient for an inclusion edge |
| **Extrinsic inclusion dependence** | Within a *named TreatFlow family context*, any coherent application containing A needs B for A's **intended product role** | Phase 006 may accept `A → B` only after purpose-based counterexamples and explanation |
| **Scope selection** | A coherent/dependence-valid subset is actually within the adopted design mandate | Deliberate product-purpose decision, separate from graph-validity |

This gate records **candidate questions**, never conclusions. In particular `Research Provision → Controlled Disclosure`, `Revision → Observation`, and `Contribution Stewardship → Observation` must **not** be accepted solely from existing Phase 005 C/D composition links.

## 3. Candidate inclusion questions and falsification probes

| Candidate question | Counterexample / alternative subset to test | Risk of an unjustified edge |
|---|---|---|
| Is Observation required by every variant? | Can a scoped evidence-fitness inquiry, generic correction history, or protected non-observation material have value without active Halloween reporting? | A product mandate is not a universal concept law |
| Is Stewardship required whenever reporting exists? | Distracted observer reports valid group evidence without identity; C-01 is optional | Compulsory registration contradicts P-01 |
| Is Revision required whenever Observation is included? | A bounded historical record may remain interpretable without amendment capability; examine P-05 cost and accepted scope | C-02 does not mean mandatory co-inclusion |
| Does Revision require Observation in the TreatFlow family? | Generic factual assertion histories may have other meaningful targets; which are legitimately in this project's scope? | Confusing generic concept purpose with family-specific role |
| Does Research Provision always require Controlled Disclosure? | A suitability-only, no-delivery inquiry may return unsuitable/inconclusive without access | D-02 delivery coupling does not create universal dependency |
| Does protected research delivery require Disclosure? | D-02 guards `Provide` and `MakeAvailable`; can a limited variant omit delivery? | Delivery requires protection, but whole Research Provision may not |
| Is disclosure meaningful without Research Provision? | Protected nonresearch material may need sharing/restriction decisions | Scientific suitability is not consent or release authority |
| Does corrected evidence require Stewardship? | Independently entitled amender may legitimately act without public person association | Standing and amendment authority are distinct |
| What if all five are present? | Full guarded application action surface, C and D participation | Co-presence does not prove mutual dependence |
| Can minimal observation-only satisfy TreatFlow's entire product purpose? | P-03/P-04 and reader expectations may fail despite coherent reporting | Dependence validity ≠ strategic/full-purpose acceptance |

Test genuine variants that feel unfamiliar, **including zero-delivery research evaluation, protected nonresearch disclosure, and restricted correction-only history**. Do not fabricate realistic demand or rights for an abstract possibility.

## 4. Analysis semantics for subsequent subphases

Use a concise **directed concept-inclusion relation** only after evidence warrants it: `A → B` means every coherent subset in the *specified family* that includes A also includes B for A's intended application role. Preserve context, purpose rationale, decisive missing-role counterexample and whether edge is conditional. Compute/describe transitive closures only when useful; test cycles as possible co-inclusion rather than automatic errors.

For each illustrative subset, distinguish:
- **dependence-valid** by accepted edges (which do not yet exist);
- **coherent in a stated context** for its intended purpose;
- **adopted/in scope** by a deliberate mandate decision;
- **coherent but out of scope**;
- **invalid because of an accepted dependency**;
- **unresolved** due to absent evidence.

A mathematically valid empty subset need not be useful. Do not enumerate all 32 theoretical subsets merely to fill a document; select discriminating cases and any useful transitive/cycle evidence. Avoid naming a universal MVP, price tier, implementation package or rollout strategy.

### 006-F — Consolidation, Documentation Integrity, Exit Review & Phase 007 Handoff

**Purpose:** independent audit under [Phase 006 exit template](exit-review-template.md): accepted contextual edges, proof/counterexamples, representative subsets, optionality/cycles, variants, scope and composition effects, authority and OKF coherence. **Inputs:** 006-B–E and current canonical knowledge. **Evidence:** PASS/PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD/NOT READY, residuals and explicit next Phase 007 start-gate eligibility. **Gate:** no architecture/implementation; exit only on coherent dependency family and discoverable authority.

**Dependency order:** B establishes warranted edges; C tests their consequences and alternatives; D chooses intentional project scope; E reconciles composed behavior by variant; F independently exits. Reopen earlier phases as defects demand. No speculative parallel canonical documents.
