# TreatFlow — 002-A Discovery Scope, Divergence Strategy, Candidate Criteria & Subphase Planning

## Gate decision

**READY TO BEGIN PHASE 002 SUBPHASES.** The [Phase 001 exit](../001/001-e-treatflow-exit-review.md) is **PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD**, and the [current purpose baseline](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) identifies discriminating obligations P-01–P-06 and eight evaluative situations. This gate authorizes **candidate exploration only**. No candidate is yet selected, and Phase 003/implementation are not authorized.

## 1. Discovery authority and evidence posture

The [current project intake](../../canonical/project/intake.md) owns sponsor premises, contextual risks, and open scope questions. The [current purpose model](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) owns the product purpose, actor burdens, P-01–P-06 and success framing. [Phase 001 records](../001/) explain historical reasoning, but are not competing current truth.

Relevant evidence: sponsor-stated interest in voluntary Halloween reporting, trustworthy analysis and protected data release; inferred burden and privacy concerns; hypothetical purpose-level success situations. **Absent:** interviews, pilot measurements, demonstrated demand, legal/compliance conclusions, privacy disclosure assessment, and validated statistical assumptions. Alternative ideas discovered by analogy, imagination, existing products or LLM assistance remain proposals—not observations or requirements.

## 2. De-anchoring risks and controls

- Do not promote words such as **Arrival, Group, Household, Session, Account, Event, Dataset, Download** directly into a concept catalog. Nouns may denote domain context, data or relationships instead of end-to-end behavior.
- Do not assume separate “logging”, “timer”, “export”, “login” and “dashboard” concepts merely because the sponsor suggested collection, secure identity and analysis.
- Do not conflate **one group encounter** with several independent visitor arrivals or a **missing interval** with an observed zero.
- Do not require a Poisson-oriented visualization or model-confirmation feature; preserving the ability to reject the hypothesis fulfills P-03.
- Do not privilege either unrestricted download or persistent identifiable accounts before privacy/authority tradeoffs have been evaluated.
- Deliberately explore behavior allocations and generic mental models before assigning familiar implementation names. Preserve rejected alternatives as historical evidence rather than quietly deleting them.

## 3. Divergent sources and material breadth

The plan will use: (a) [P-01–P-06](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) as separate provocations; (b) the eight Phase 001 success/failure situations, particularly corrections, quiet-versus-absent observation, grouped arrivals and nonparticipating household exposure; (c) familiar mental constructs such as participation, recording, evidence, controlled sharing and correction **as analogy prompts only**; (d) alternative viewpoints of volunteer, analyst, operator and nonparticipant; (e) failures and value tensions as candidate-discovery prompts.

Meaningful divergence requires **materially different distributions of behavior**, not a quota of names. Explore, where plausible:
- **Observation framing:** live event-oriented activity versus bounded-period accounting versus other credible reporting structures, without assuming any is a required concept.
- **Identity/authority framing:** contributor-centered accountability versus submission-centered provenance versus separable privacy/participation responsibilities.
- **Research access framing:** direct access to shareable records versus mediated or purpose-limited disclosure versus derived summaries; no candidate is an approved release policy.
- **Quality framing:** correction incorporated with contribution versus independently understandable change/revision behavior.
- **Genericity/familiarity:** domain-specific Halloween semantics versus familiar reusable concepts where they support independence without losing purpose.

No one of these examples establishes a final concept or an approved decomposition. Breadth is sufficient only when materially different behavioral ownership and tradeoffs can be compared.

## 4. Criteria and candidate evidence

During **divergence**, the Jackson lenses—user-facing, semantic, independent, behavioral, purposive, end-to-end, familiar, reusable—are orientation, **not** a rejection rubric. Weak or strange seeds may remain for exploration.

During **convergence**, a retained candidate must plausibly embody a recognizable mental construct and coherent end-to-end behavior; show credible association with at least one of P-01–P-06; have alternatives/boundaries considered; not merely be a UI widget, static datum, CRUD step, technical service or storage artifact; and visibly state concerns requiring Phase 003/004 work. Use dispositions **Exploring**, **Retained for specification**, **Reframe/split/merge candidate**, **Deferred**, **Rejected**. Do not invent quantitative maturity scores or pretend to complete state/action specification or factoring.

Lightweight phase records for significant candidates should identify working label, motivating purpose(s), idea source, behavior sketch, alternative decomposition, concept-likeness concerns, uncertainty and current disposition. Generation provenance is *not* proof.

## 5. Dependency-safe project-specific sequence

### 002-B — Divergent Seeds & Purpose/Scenario Exploration

**Mode:** protected divergence. **Inputs:** canonical purpose/intake, Phase 001 situations, discovery contract. **Questions:** what different recognizable behaviors might address each purpose; what do affected nonusers reveal; where are familiar analogies misleading? **Evidence:** one consolidated phase-based seed inventory, purpose/scenario references, candidate origins and unanswered questions, without screening every seed. **Canonical:** none by default. **Excludes:** selection, detailed state/actions, implementation. **Completion:** every P-01–P-06 receives plausible exploration, including safety-centered and nonstandard seeds, with no premature filtering. **Handoff:** seed inventory to C.

### 002-C — Alternative Conceptual Decompositions & Boundary Experiments

**Mode:** continued divergence plus explicit alternative framing, with limited critical comparison. **Inputs:** B and authoritative purpose model. **Questions:** which behaviors can be grouped, separated, made generic, or modeled independently; which competing decompositions genuinely differ in semantics rather than names? **Evidence:** concise comparisons of distinct concept-boundary allocations and their consequences, split/merge/genericity questions, affected-party tradeoffs. **Canonical:** none until semantic identity stabilizes. **Excludes:** definitive concept catalog and full concept specification. **Completion:** serious alternatives in observation, participation/accountability, correction and access/release are compared, not just renamed. **Handoff:** comparison evidence to D.

### 002-D — Concept-Likeness Review, Purpose Coverage & Reframing

**Mode:** convergence begins. **Inputs:** B/C evidence and concept criteria. **Questions:** which candidates have user-facing, semantic, behavioral, independent and end-to-end potential; which are mere entities/features/technical means; which purposes have gaps? **Evidence:** candidate-to-purpose coverage and reasoned disposition/reframe records; named residual doubts. **Canonical:** provisional concept knowledge only if a sufficiently stable identity independently merits it. **Excludes:** complete operational principles, state/action models and Phase 004 proof. **Completion:** purpose gaps and misleading candidates are exposed; questionable candidates are reframed or assigned next-step questions. **Handoff:** review to E.

### 002-E — Bounded Convergence & Candidate Handoff Preparation

**Mode:** disciplined convergence, reopening B/C if gaps warrant. **Inputs:** D, alternatives and purposes. **Questions:** which plausible candidates deserve behavioral specification and why; which alternatives must be preserved or deferred; what remains uncertain? **Evidence:** retained-for-specification shortlist, purpose association, short mental-model/behavior sketches, disposition rationale and explicit Phase 003/004 boundary questions. **Canonical:** create the smallest coherent provisional concept owner only for semantically stable retained candidates; avoid a document per brainstormed seed. **Excludes:** final approval, architecture, synchronization or UI mapping. **Completion:** Phase 003 could understand what to specify without treating candidates as proven. **Handoff:** evidence to F.

### 002-F — Consolidation, Integrity Audit, Exit Review & Phase 003 Handoff

**Mode:** independent exit/consolidation under the [exit-review template](exit-review-template.md). **Inputs:** all B–E evidence and applicable authority. **Questions:** was discovery truly divergent; are P-01–P-06 covered; are retained candidates concept-shaped; were significant alternatives considered; is documentation current, linked and nonduplicative? **Evidence:** PASS / PASS WITH CARRY-FORWARD / NOT READY TO EXIT; retained/deferred/rejected candidate reasoning; uncertainty and Phase 003 handoff. **Canonical:** only legitimately retained stable candidate meaning; phase history remains historical. **Excludes:** Phase 003 execution or any feature implementation.

**Dependency rationale:** B generates without premature judgment; C develops materially different boundaries; D applies qualified criteria; E converges only as much as required for behavioral specification; F reviews completeness and knowledge integrity. Reopen divergence if D/E expose coverage gaps; do not use document count as success.

## 6. Canonical authority, OKF and indexes

Do not rewrite [canonical purpose](../../canonical/purpose/purposes.md) or [project intake](../../canonical/project/intake.md) into candidate documents. Treat divergent and rejected seeds as **phase evidence**. A provisional `docs/canonical/concepts/` entry is appropriate only in E (or demonstrably stable earlier work), and must be clearly labeled retained-for-specification, not finalized.

The Phase 002 index should expose execution records and later retained candidates as linked navigation; update `docs/canonical/index.md` only if durable candidate meaning is created; update root `docs/index.md` for lifecycle status without duplicating purpose rules. Ordinary knowledge documents require OKF `type` frontmatter; non-root `index.md` remains navigational. Check path/link integrity and current-versus-historical authority on each subphase.

## 7. Phase 003 handoff expectations, uncertainties and safeguards

Phase 002 exit must provide a tractable **provisional** candidate set, tentative P-01–P-06 associations, mental-model/behavior sketches, meaningful contrasting decompositions, historical rejected/deferred records, reasons for convergence, and open purpose, independence, boundary, end-to-end, genericity, familiarity, and authority questions assigned to Phase 003/004. Phase 003 remains free to revise or reject candidates.

Carry forward empirically untested participation burdens, event/unit/observation exposure, scientific bias, contributor age/consent, identity-versus-site/linkability, geographic detail, disclosure/release rules, retention/withdrawal, and operator capacity. These remain **unresolved**, and privacy/consent/data-release qualification is a **prelaunch obligation**; Phase 002 candidate exploration does not authorize personal-data collection.
