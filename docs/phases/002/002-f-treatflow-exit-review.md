## 2. Divergence and anchoring integrity

The discovery did not merely rename sponsor features. Distinct behavioral models include event diary versus monitored-period accounting versus bounded evidence claims versus separate exposure/observation; contributor-centered versus evidence-centered provenance; correction-in-reporting versus independent revision versus protected withdrawal versus operator challenge; and shareable evidence versus controlled access versus derived summaries versus external or integrated analysis.

The five whole-application thought experiments W-A–W-E differ in behavioral responsibilities and stakeholder costs, **not** only interface wording. The resulting five retained candidates were not copied from the 28 seed names as a 1:1 subset, and no candidate count was treated as an optimization target.

Negative controls remain explicit: group, visitor, arrival, timestamp, confidence, location classification, account/login, CSV, dashboard, dataset container, Poisson computation and technical components have **no standalone concept authority** merely because they appear in the intake.

**Assessment:** divergence and independent-convergence rationale sufficient for Phase 003 entry. Generated ideas, analogies and sponsor statements remain hypotheses, not evidence of user demand.

## 4. Candidate concept-likeness audit

| Retained working identity | Exit finding | Why worth Phase 003 specification | Critical challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | **Plausible** | Recognizable record/coverage behavior for an observer; end-to-end contribution value | Exposure as intrinsic behavior or separate concept; grouped arrivals, remembered claims, unknown coverage |
| **Contribution stewardship** | **Questionable but worth specifying** | Possible contributor-facing ability to revisit and control attributable contributions | May conflate login, participation, provenance and correction with no independent end-to-end promise |
| **Revision** | **Questionable but worth specifying** | Meaningful factual repair and intelligible history for contributors/researchers | Could be only an action of Observation; challenge versus correction authority |
| **Controlled disclosure** | **Plausible** | Familiar governed act of sharing with recognizable beneficiary and protective constraints | Might be only a policy over Research provision; responsibility for nonparticipants |
| **Research provision** | **Questionable but worth specifying** | Meaningful analytical access to scoped evidence and limitations | May be a data container/output of disclosure rather than independent user-facing behavior |

No candidate is rated **Not ready for Phase 003** because each questionable candidate has a precise testable hypothesis rather than only an entity, UI or technical structure. This is a threshold for *further challenge*, not concept validation. Phase 003 must be free to split, merge, reframe, defer or reject.

## 6. Specification/implementation boundary audit

No candidate has an approved Jackson operational principle, abstract state machine, action set, preconditions/postconditions, invariants, concepts synchronization, app dependency graph, authority allocation, UI mapping, database schema, API, infrastructure or implementation. Phase 003 is specifically responsible for **purpose, operational principle, abstract state and actions**. Phase 004 owns stronger factoring/independence/genericity scrutiny; later phases own cross-concept synchronizations and authority/experience design.

Particular warnings: **Withdrawal of future data use is not retroactive erasure from third-party downloads**; **non-reporting is not an observed zero**; **a group of five is not five independent arrival events**; **coarse geography plus fine timestamps can still expose households**. These are concerns requiring explicit conceptual semantics, not instructions to implement a chosen mechanism.
