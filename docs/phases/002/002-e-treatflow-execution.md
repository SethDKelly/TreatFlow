## 1. Convergence rationale

We did not simply retain all twelve plausible seeds from 002-D. We sought the smallest **plausible**, not proven, set of distinct user-understandable behavioral hypotheses that can cover the research, contribution and protection purposes without hiding difficult questions.

| Working retained candidate | Seed inspirations and alternatives considered | Reason worth specifying; risk carried forward |
|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | S-01/02/03/05/06; O-A diary, O-B watched-period accounting, O-C bounded claim, O-D split exposure | A contributor-recognizable record of observed activity can potentially cover low burden and truthful exposure. Critical: quiet versus unobserved intervals and groups must remain intelligible; split exposure remains open |
| **Contribution stewardship** | S-09/10/23; I-A contributor-centered, I-B claim provenance, I-C separable participation, I-D scoped identity | May enable protected attribution/revisiting and meaningful participation control without public stable identifiers. Critical: avoid merging three unrelated concerns into an oversized concept |
| **Revision** | S-11/13/28; C-A correction intrinsic to reporting, C-B independent revision, C-D steward challenge | Factual amendment/history may have its own recognizable end-to-end value, distinct from the initial act of observing. Critical: test true independence rather than an arbitrary CRUD operation |
| **Controlled disclosure** | S-12/16/17/19/24; R-A combined release, R-B separate review/access, R-C summaries | Information sharing under protective conditions is a familiar mental construct; third-party risks and authorization must remain explicit. Critical: policy may be an intrinsic guard, not a standalone concept |
| **Research provision** | S-15/20/21/22/27; R-A shareable evidence, R-D external analysis, R-E built-in inquiry | Providing interpretable, scoped observational evidence is plausibly valuable beyond granting permission; statistical conclusions can remain with outside analysts. Critical: may collapse into disclosure if no independent user-facing behavior emerges |

These are *behavior sketches*, not completed operational principles. Each must still pass specification/factoring tests for semantic independence, coherent lifecycle and genericity.

## 2. Candidate decisions on the unretained paths

- **Reframe within or across retained candidates:** S-03 periodic tally, S-05 recall and S-14 confidence as observation modes/qualifiers; S-04 group encounter as measurement unit; S-08 household context as possibly sensitive contextual relation; S-10 provenance as a quality/accountability concern; S-13 duplicates and S-28 challenges as possible revision/stewardship behavior; S-17 disclosure review and S-22 qualification as conditions of research sharing. These are *not* individually promoted.
- **Deferred pending evidence/scope:** S-07 pledge, S-18 community summary, S-20 notebook, S-25 bounded campaign, S-26 observer handoff, S-27 privacy comparison as distinct concept; S-19 independent access grant, S-23 independent participant-choice behavior and S-24 independent operator intervention remain **reopenable** if the five candidates demonstrably cannot fulfill protected purposes.
- **Rejected as standalone in current framing:** S-04 group, S-14 confidence, S-21 rate calculation; also login, CSV, dashboard, database entities and exact timestamp fields. This rejects *independent concept status*, not domain relevance.
- **Do not collapse factual correction with future-use withdrawal:** S-12 may belong to protected participation/disclosure and must be tested for a separate legitimate user purpose. Already released third-party copies cannot be assumed recoverable.

Earlier 002-D statuses remain historical screening results; these refined dispositions govern the **002-E provisional shortlist**, not all candidate futures.

## 3. Purpose-coverage challenge, including negative controls

| Purpose | Provisional coverage | False-completion test and unresolved risk |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 Low-burden participation | Observation | If the only supported claim demands precise continuous door monitoring, P-01 fails; alternate report forms must remain possible to examine |
| P-02 Interpretable observations | Observation; Revision; Research provision | If unknown exposure appears as zero or group visitors appear as independent arrivals, P-02 fails |
| P-03 Honest research | Research provision; Controlled disclosure | If the recipient obtains records without time/exposure/sampling limitations, or the system implies Poisson confirmation, **P-03 remains unmet** |
| P-04 Nonparticipant protection | Controlled disclosure; Contribution stewardship | If release links sparse timing to a recognizable household or children, P-04 fails regardless of analyst benefits |
| P-05 Accountable correction | Contribution stewardship; Revision | If one observer cannot responsibly correct a claim, or corrections publicly identify them, P-05 fails |
| P-06 Sustainable stewardship | Controlled disclosure; Revision; bounded contribution controls | If maintaining grants, appeals or takedowns requires an unrealistic standing team, **P-06 remains unmet** |

No purpose is simply checked off because its title appears beside a concept; scenario failures can reopen divergence in 002-F, 003 or 004.

## 4. Comparison of whole-application decompositions

- W-A diary-first is not adopted unchanged: active monitored exposure must not become an accidental implicit zero.
- W-B period-first informs **Observation**, but a formal campaign/session is not automatically required.
- W-C claim-centered informed provenance and revisions, but five candidates are not mandated as one generic claim platform.
- W-D privacy-first informs **Controlled disclosure** without forfeiting the sponsor's exploratory dataset purpose.
- W-E seasonal study remains contextual unless study/campaign behavior independently earns its place.

This is bounded convergence from *contrasting behavior allocations*, not winner selection by seed popularity or ease of coding.

## 5. Phase 003/004 question queue

1. Can Observation encompass active-interval coverage, grouped encounter semantics, recalled claims, and correction as one coherent behavior, or must the boundary change?
2. Does Contribution stewardship offer a standalone mental model beyond participant identity, provenance metadata and ownership permissions? Could it be scoped without irreversible public linkage?
3. Is Revision an independent end-to-end concept or just an operation inside Observation? Who may challenge versus correct, and how is correction history understood?
4. Is controlled disclosure a user-facing lifecycle or a policy over Research provision? How are audience restrictions and nonparticipant protections made credible?
5. Does Research provision have a distinct behavioral lifecycle from disclosure and a purpose beyond presenting a container of records? Where do measurement limitations belong?
6. Are informed choice, withdrawal of future use and steward intervention missing independent concepts? Assess protected actor authority and third-party interests; do not silently exclude them for minimalism.
7. How are monitored zeros, missing exposure, group/person counts, temporal uncertainty, nonrandom sample bias and disclosure linkage represented without assuming architecture?
8. What are the likely familiar/reusable mental constructs, and where does genericity obscure the Halloween-specific scientific need?

Phase 003 should reject, split or merge candidates if behavioral specification fails. Phase 004 performs strong factoring tests; authority, synchronization and user-visible mapping belong to their later phases.
