## 4. Unresolved distinctions and de-anchoring challenges

1. Is an *arrival* a real-world occurrence, a reported measurement, or a user-facing behavioral construct? Do not equate visitors, groups and encounters.
2. Could a coherent *observation* behavior include activity, exposure and correction, or do these have independent purposes?
3. Is *participation* meaningful behavior beyond identity and report submission? Can a person observe without an enduring account?
4. Is *provenance* part of contribution, an independent claim lifecycle, or a concern of research access?
5. Do *release*, *access*, *privacy screening* and *summaries* form one familiar sharing construct or several independent behaviors?
6. Is analysis part of TreatFlow's product purpose or primarily work done by external dataset consumers?
7. Does a request to withdraw already-released data imply an impossible promise? Separate future disclosure control from irreversible historical access.
8. How much operator-facing intervention is warranted before the small seasonal project becomes unmanageable?
