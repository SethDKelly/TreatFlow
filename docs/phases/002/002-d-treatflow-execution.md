## 1. Calibrated concept-likeness test

A promising candidate should plausibly be **user-facing, semantic, behavioral, purposive, end-to-end and independently understandable**. Familiarity and general reuse are useful questions, not demands to turn every behavior into a generic framework. Phase 002-D cannot establish completed operational principles, state/actions, independence proof or full factoring; those belong to 003–004.

Use:
- **Plausible — examine at 002-E:** conceivable coherent end-to-end behavior serving a purpose, with boundaries still open.
- **Reframe/split/merge candidate:** idea carries useful behavior but names a property, fragment, overlapping concern, or unclear independent unit.
- **Deferred:** idea may be coherent but scope, evidence, and viability do not yet justify foregrounding it.
- **Rejected as standalone candidate:** idea is a domain entity, measurement field, UI/technical mechanism or external method *in its current form*. Underlying need remains valid and may be addressed elsewhere.

These are not numerical scores. **Plausible** does not mean retained.

## 5. Specific reframing questions for 002-E

1. Could **one observation behavior** handle live, aggregate and recalled reports *and* recognized active exposure, or does exposure independently merit a concept?
2. Does **contribution identity** offer an independent end-to-end user-facing behavior beyond login/association metadata?
3. Can **revision**, **withdrawal** and **challenge** be understood with distinct protected purposes without requiring three heavyweight concepts?
4. Is a **research-ready collection** a meaningful research provision behavior, or merely the records delivered by a separate sharing concept?
5. Does **qualified interpretation** require product behavior, or can TreatFlow responsibly supply evidence/limitations for external research?
6. What safeguards for **nonparticipating households and children** must constrain any disclosure mechanism, independent of concept naming?
7. Can a proposed **research access grant** or **steward intervention** be justified by the needs of a minimal seasonal project?
8. Which generic/familiar concepts fit naturally, versus abstractions that obscure Halloween measurement semantics?
